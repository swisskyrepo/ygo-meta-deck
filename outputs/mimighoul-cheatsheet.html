Flip-donation control — checked June 7, 2026

Mimighoul

A control deck that donates face-down Flip monsters, turns their bad effects against the controller, and wins by trapping the opponent under Mimighoul Dungeon while looping Dragon, Archfiend, Room, Master, and Rank 1 pressure.

Last checked: 2026-06-07 Role: resource-denial control Win: Dungeon lock + hand rip + Rank 1 Formats: TCG / Master Duel / Genesys

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Mimighoul is a control engine, not a pure combo race. You give the opponent face-down monsters, then flip them so you receive the upside while they suffer the lock, discard, board wipe, or activation restriction. If the opponent controls your set Mimighoul, its Flip text is read from their perspective: Mimighoul Dragon destroys their face-up non-Mimighouls, Mimighoul Archfiend makes them lose a card while you draw, Slime/Flower let you search or summon, and Fairy restricts their hand-summoned monster effects.

  • 1Never give without a flip plan. End with Master, Room, Maker in GY, Number 31, Treasure Panther, or Book effects.
  • 2Do not overfill zones. You need one set monster for Dungeon; extra donated monsters are leverage, not decoration.
  • 3Respect Maker timing. If you used Maker this turn, the GY flip waits until the opponent's turn.

Win Condition And Format Notes

Primary win: keep Mimighoul Dungeon online while the opponent controls a set monster. They lose their Normal Summon and cannot attack with monsters Special Summoned that turn, buying turns for Archfiend hand pressure and Rank 1 bodies.

Closing: convert repeated Level 1s into Giant Mimighoul, Throne/Master pressure, Lyrilusc ZEUS lines, or variant payoffs like Utopic Future Zexal, Yummy Synchros, Life Hack damage, or Bot Herder swings.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Dungeon first when you need the missing monster. Dungeon turns two-card hands into deterministic lines.
  • 2Dragon cannot fix everything. Dragon's Flip wipe is Main Phase only, and if the opponent flips it early you lose that disruption window.
  • 3Archfiend is the choke point. Archfiend's Flip draw/discard can be Ash'd, so do not spend every extender before it resolves unless the payoff demands it.
  • 4Room before your own wipe. Bounce Mimighoul cards with Room's GY effect before ZEUS or other board wipes when possible.
  • 5Stop at the lock against hand traps. Dungeon + one opponent set + Room is often better than extending into Nibiru or extra Mulcharmy draws.

Recovery Options

  • 1Dungeon adds from GY. Rebuy Dragon, Archfiend, Fairy, or an Extra Deck Mimighoul that returns to the Extra Deck instead of hand.
  • 2Room GY bounces any number. Recycle face-up Mimighouls, save Master/Throne, dodge targeted negation, or reload donated monsters.
  • 3Giant searches any Mimighoul card. Use spare Level 1s to replace a stopped Dungeon, Maker, or Room.
  • 4Master searches a monster on summon. It turns Throne, Slime, or revival lines into follow-up even after your first wave is broken.
  • 5Flower rebuilds from any face-down. If a set monster remains, Flower is both an extender and a search when flipped on the opponent's field.

Mirror / rogue control Cosmic Cyclone, Shared Ride, Magic Deflector, Summoning Curse, Bot Herder for stolen board states Avoid giving the mirror clean Archfiend or Flower value. If you can answer their Dungeon, their monsters become much less oppressive.

What Stops You

  • 1Removing Dungeon: without it, opponents play through the single set monster.
  • 2Ash Blossom on Archfiend's flip draw/discard — the line's choke point.
  • 3Mass backrow removal (Cosmic, Trunade-style) hitting Dungeon/Room before the recycle.
  • 4GY banishing that removes Room and Maker triggers.
  • 5Opponents flipping the donated Dragon early or using your face-downs as their own resources.

How You Counter Back

  • 1Room's GY bounce dodges targeted negation and reloads donated monsters.
  • 2Giant searches any Mimighoul card to replace whatever was stopped.
  • 3Dungeon rebuys from GY, so single removal rarely ends the lock.
  • 4The donated Dragon Flip wipe answers established big boards going second.
  • 5Flower/Panther rebuild from any remaining face-down monster.
