Playstyle and Win Condition

Control first, pressure second. The deck wins by making every removal trade feed another set trap, body, or Rank 8 threat.

Eldlich Horus

Keep the opponent inside narrow lanes with traps, then turn recurring Zombies and Horus monsters into Rank 8/10 pressure or protected damage.

Backrow loop GY bodies Rank toolbox Super Poly pivots

1 Eldlich axis

Scarlet Sanguine summons Golden Lord, Golden Land traps become Zombies, then Conquistador, Huaquero, and Glorious Golden Land trade while replacing themselves in the End Phase.

2 Horus axis

Imsety finds King's Sarcophagus, Sarcophagus loads Horus names, and each name revives itself from the GY while Sarcophagus is controlled.

3 Lock axis

Skill Drain, There Can Be Only One, Dragon's Mind, Golden Land Forever, and floodgate side cards trade well because your engines work from hand, GY, or trap bodies.

4 Close axis

Rank 8 negates, 3500 protected Eldlich, Super Polymerization, Gustav Max burn, and Varudras convert a simplified game into lethal pressure.

Sequencing Tips

High-value details for avoiding self-inflicted locks and wasting recursive cards.

Openers

  • If you have Cursed Eldland and Imsety, lead Cursed Eldland first. If it eats Ash, Imsety is still live; if it resolves, the card you add becomes Imsety discard fuel.
  • Activate Rank/Link plans before Scarlet Sanguine if Scarlet will resolve this turn, because Scarlet locks later Special Summons to Zombies.
  • Do not dump all four Horus names by default. Two names usually make a Rank 8; four names are for Giantrainer reload or very high-pressure hands.
  • Dragon's Mind needs a monster with 2500 original ATK or DEF. Eldlich, Imsety, Number 38, Number 90, and Varudras qualify.

Resource Use

  • Discard Eldlixir or Golden Land cards to Imsety and Super Polymerization when their delayed GY set effect matters more than keeping them in hand.
  • Flip Skill Drain after resolving effects that need to matter on field. GY and hand effects still keep the deck functional, but Horus trigger effects on field become blank.
  • King's Sarcophagus battle send is non-destruction removal during the Damage Step. It forces awkward combat math even through many established boards.
  • Use Huaquero proactively on cards that would otherwise be revived, fused, banished for cost, or shuffled back for follow-up.

Meta Calls

  • Current TCG tops reward cards that compress interaction: Droll, Dimensional Barrier, Anti-Spell, Super Polymerization, Evenly Matched, and Lightning Storm all have clear jobs.
  • Against Kewl Tune, Branded, Dracotail, and other engine-heavy decks, commit to one high-impact stop instead of trading three small traps into an already established chain.
  • Against Sky Striker and control mirrors, hold Cosmic Cyclone or Huaquero for recursion points instead of firing them into replaceable cards.
  • Against Maliss and other banish decks, Lancea is a cleaner turn-skip card than trying to win a long value exchange.

Common Misplays

  • Sending your only live set trap for Eldlich GY effect without checking whether the End Phase replacement chain will still exist.
  • Making Coach King Giantrainer when you need Battle Phase damage that same turn.
  • Letting King's Sarcophagus get banished for free. If Sarcophagus is gone, Horus bodies stop self-summoning.
  • Activating Dominus Purge from hand without accepting the rest-of-duel DARK, WATER, and FIRE monster-effect lock.

Recovery Options

The deck is built to be annoying after a board wipe. Recovery depends on what zone still has cards.

GY recovery

Eldlich returns by sending a Spell/Trap you control. Scarlet and Golden Land traps banish themselves in the End Phase to set the opposite half of the engine. Hapi can pull two cards from GY or banishment when the opponent removes your card.

Sarcophagus recovery

If King's Sarcophagus survives, every Horus name in GY threatens to become material again next turn. This makes discard costs and board wipes less punishing.

Spell/Trap recovery

Delta can return to hand when a monster is added from GY to hand. Glorious Golden Land can reset banished Golden Land or Eldlixir cards. Canopic Protector and Dragon's Mind set themselves back with restrictions.

Side Deck Options and Counters

Side for the current field, and know how opponents will attack this deck.

Side in by matchup

  • Combo/Fusion fields: Dimensional Barrier, Super Polymerization, Droll & Lock Bird, Dominus Purge, and Dragon's Mind.
  • Banish/Cyberse fields: Artifact Lancea, Ghost Belle, Droll, and timely Huaquero. Use Lancea to stop the turn, not to trade small.
  • Backrow/control mirrors: Lord of the Heavenly Prison, Cosmic Cyclone, Harpie's Feather Duster, Lightning Storm, and Evenly Matched.
  • Established monster boards: Lava Golem, Dark Hole, Evenly Matched, Super Polymerization, and TY-PHON/Zeus lines.
  • Spell-heavy decks: Anti-Spell Fragrance going first, backed by a body that turns Dragon's Mind online.

Likely counters against you

  • Ash Blossom: Imsety, Cursed Eldland, and Scarlet Sanguine are common targets.
  • Droll & Lock Bird: Punishes Imsety search plus draw and any follow-up search chain.
  • Ghost Belle / D.D. Crow: Interrupt Eldlich, Scarlet, Horus self-summons, and Golden Land recursion points.
  • Cosmic Cyclone: Banishing Scarlet, Golden Land traps, or King's Sarcophagus cuts off replacement value.
  • Shifter, Macro effects, Necrovalley, Soul Drain: Attack the GY loop directly and force you into normal trap control.
  • Evenly, Duster, Lightning Storm: Make Lord of the Heavenly Prison, Golden Land Forever, Dragon's Mind, or conservative set counts matter.
