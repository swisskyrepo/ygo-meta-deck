Trap-control grind — checked June 8, 2026

Eldlich Horus

A top-to-bottom cheatsheet for piloting the trap grind engine, the Horus Level 8 package, and the modern Golden Land support seen in recent tournament and rogue variants. Control first, pressure second: every removal trade should feed another set trap, body, or Rank 8 threat.

Checked June 8, 2026 Role: trap attrition control Win: Rank 8/10 + protected Eldlich swings Rogue TCG shell — latest top Oct 25, 2025

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Keep the opponent inside narrow lanes with traps, then turn recurring Zombies and Horus monsters into Rank 8/10 pressure or protected damage. The deck runs on four axes:

  • 1Eldlich axis: Eldlixir of Scarlet Sanguine summons the Golden Lord, Golden Land traps become Zombies, then Conquistador of the Golden Land, Huaquero of the Golden Land, and Eldlixir of the Glorious Golden Land trade while replacing themselves in the End Phase.
  • 2Horus axis: Imsety, Glory of Horus finds King's Sarcophagus, Sarcophagus loads Horus names, and each name revives itself from the GY while Sarcophagus is controlled.
  • 3Lock axis: Skill Drain, There Can Be Only One, Dragon's Mind, and Golden Land Forever! trade well because your engines work from hand, GY, or trap bodies.
  • 4Close axis: Rank 8 negates, a 3500 protected Eldlich the Golden Lord, Super Polymerization, Superdreadnought Rail Cannon Gustav Max burn, and Varudras, the Final Bringer of the End Times convert a simplified game into lethal pressure.

Win Condition And Format Notes

Status: rogue TCG control shell. Latest directly relevant tournament top found: Guayaquil WCQ Regional Top 4 on October 25, 2025 (43-card Horus Eldlich).

Meta pressure: the June 2026 field is shaped by Kewl Tune (27.44% of last-month TCG tops), Branded (16.64%), DoomZ (10.12%), Dracotail (5.83%), plus Elfnote, Mitsurugi, Sky Striker, Maliss, Yummy, and Vanquish Soul K9.

  • 1Win by attrition: every set trap, Eldlich revival, and Horus self-summon must trade up or replace itself.
  • 2Close with Rank 8 negates (Number 90: Galaxy-Eyes Photon Lord, Number 38: Hope Harbinger Dragon Titanic Galaxy), Rank 10 burn, Super Polymerization pivots, and protected Eldlich swings.
  • 3Floodgates are real win conditions here: your engines keep working under Skill Drain and There Can Be Only One while most opponents do not.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1With Cursed Eldland and Imsety, lead Cursed Eldland. If it eats Ash Blossom, Imsety is still live; if it resolves, the searched card becomes Imsety discard fuel.
  • 2Resolve Rank/Link plans before Scarlet Sanguine if it will resolve this turn — Scarlet locks later Special Summons to Zombies.
  • 3Do not dump all four Horus names by default. Two names usually make a Rank 8; four are for the Giantrainer reload or very high-pressure hands.
  • 4Flip Skill Drain after resolving effects that must matter on field. GY and hand effects keep the deck functional, but Horus trigger effects on field go blank.
  • 5King's Sarcophagus battle send is non-destruction removal during the Damage Step — it forces awkward combat math even through established boards.
  • 6Use Huaquero proactively on cards that would otherwise be revived, fused, banished for cost, or shuffled back for follow-up.

Common Misplays

  • 1Sending your only live set trap for Eldlich's GY effect without checking whether the End Phase replacement chain will still exist.
  • 2Making Coach King Giantrainer when you need Battle Phase damage that same turn.
  • 3Letting King's Sarcophagus get banished for free — without it, Horus bodies stop self-summoning.
  • 4Activating Dominus Purge from hand without accepting the rest-of-duel DARK, WATER, and FIRE monster-effect lock.

Meta Calls (June 2026)

  • 1Current TCG tops reward cards that compress interaction: Droll & Lock Bird, Dimensional Barrier, Anti-Spell Fragrance, Super Polymerization, Evenly Matched, and Lightning Storm all have clear jobs.
  • 2Against Kewl Tune, Branded, Dracotail, and other engine-heavy decks, commit one high-impact stop instead of trading three small traps into an established chain.
  • 3Against Sky Striker and control mirrors, hold Cosmic Cyclone or Huaquero for recursion points instead of firing them into replaceable cards.
  • 4Against Maliss and other banish decks, Artifact Lancea is a cleaner turn-skip than trying to win a long value exchange.

Recovery Options

  • 1GY recovery: Eldlich returns by sending a Spell/Trap you control; Scarlet and Golden Land traps banish themselves in the End Phase to set the opposite half; Hapi pulls two cards from GY or banishment when the opponent removes your card.
  • 2Sarcophagus recovery: if King's Sarcophagus survives, every Horus name in GY threatens to become material again next turn — discard costs and board wipes hurt less.
  • 3Spell/Trap recovery: Delta of Invitation returns to hand when a monster is added from GY to hand; Glorious Golden Land resets banished Golden Land or Eldlixir cards; Canopic Protector and Dragon's Mind set themselves back with restrictions; Lord of the Heavenly Prison shields the backrow.

What Stops You

  • 1Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring: Imsety, Cursed Eldland, and Scarlet Sanguine are common targets.
  • 2Droll & Lock Bird punishes the Imsety search-plus-draw and any follow-up search chain.
  • 3Ghost Belle & Haunted Mansion / D.D. Crow interrupt Eldlich, Scarlet, Horus self-summons, and Golden Land recursion points.
  • 4Cosmic Cyclone banishing Scarlet, Golden Land traps, or King's Sarcophagus cuts off replacement value.
  • 5Dimension Shifter, Macro effects, Necrovalley, and Soul Drain attack the GY loop directly and force you into normal trap control.
  • 6Evenly Matched, Harpie's Feather Duster, and Lightning Storm punish over-setting.

How You Counter Back

  • 1Against mass backrow removal, make Lord of the Heavenly Prison, Golden Land Forever!, Dragon's Mind, or conservative set counts matter.
  • 2Your engines work from hand, GY, or trap bodies, so floodgates like Skill Drain and There Can Be Only One hurt the opponent far more than you.
  • 3Keep King's Sarcophagus alive: while it stands, every Horus name in GY rebuilds the board after a wipe, and Hapi converts their removal into card advantage.
  • 4Under GY hate, pivot to pure trap control plus Super Polymerization and Rank 8 bodies until the lock card is answered.
