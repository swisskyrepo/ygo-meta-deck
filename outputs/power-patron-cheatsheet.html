Power Patron Mind Map

A Pendulum toolbox deck that turns cards placed face-up in the Extra Deck into extension, searches, recursion, and Level 12 boss access. The strongest lines route toward Nerva, Junora, and Jupiter, then cash those types in for Vidolium, Vidolia, Viraia, or Shadows.

Playstyle And Win Condition

Power Patron plays like a branching resource graph. A starter places a Pendulum body face-up in the Extra Deck, that body fires its trigger, and the next piece decides whether you extend, search interaction, recover, or move into a boss type.

1. Load Face-Up Extra

Treasure, Shade, Terminus, and self-destruction effects turn the Extra Deck into a live hand. The face-up triggers are your real starters.

2. Choose The Trigger

Nervedo summons, Jupredo searches Power Patron/DoomZ Spell/Trap, Junoldo recurs, and Vidolium searches any Power Patron card.

3. Convert To Boss Type

The monster effects destroy the Level 10 bodies and summon the matching Fusion, Synchro, or Xyz boss from the Extra Deck.

4. Cash The Payoff

Triple-type boards unlock the deck's strongest text: GY lock, protected monster activations, trap banishes, and Vidolium as a 5000 ATK closer.

Boss Payoff Rules

1
Nerva needs Artmage monster-effect support for the wipe. Nerva can rewrite an Artmage monster effect into "destroy all cards your opponent controls." Do not count a Pendulum scale effect as that support unless the specific effect is a legal monster effect.
2
Junora is strongest in Elfnote hybrid. Pure Power Patron mainly values the on-summon face-up negate. The column material lock scales harder when Elfnote monsters are involved.
3
Vidolium is both searcher and finisher. Its face-up Extra Deck trigger searches any Power Patron card, but its summon asks you to return 1 Fusion, 1 Synchro, and 1 Xyz from field/GY.

Tips

Sequencing matters more than memorizing one line. The deck rewards deciding which trigger to spend before you destroy or place a Pendulum card face-up.

Protect The First Real Search

Hands that expose multiple searches should plan around Droll. If Nervedo can summon the missing body without adding, use that route before loading extra adds.

Do Not Spend Vidolium Too Early

Vidolium is the high scale in many pure hands and also the broadest Power Patron search. Destroy it only when the scale math and next search are already covered.

Use Terminus As Both Starter And Threat

Terminus sends a Power Patron from Deck/Extra Deck and can add Medius. In GY it makes battle with a Power Patron convert into face-down banish.

Jupredo Is Interaction Access

Face-up Jupredo searches the Power Patron/DoomZ Spell/Trap suite. In scale it can trade itself plus a Power Patron/DoomZ body for a field pop.

Know When To Stop

Against board breakers, one boss plus set Solemn/Dominus and face-up Extra Deck follow-up can be better than spending everything on a fragile ceiling board.

Going Second, Break Before Scaling

Current pure lists often main Super Polymerization and board breakers. Clear floodgates or negates first, then Pendulum Summon into link or boss pressure.

Recovery Options

The deck is good at converting destroyed Pendulum bodies into face-up Extra Deck advantage, but weak hands can still stall if the first scale or search is answered.

Face-Up Extra Deck Becomes Hand

Junoldo recovers a Power Patron or Elfnote card from GY or face-up Extra Deck. Nervedo can turn face-up Extra Deck into another body. Vidolium can search the missing Power Patron card.

Graveyard Lines Stay Relevant

Terminus in GY threatens battle banish, Viraia in GY replaces future top-deck banish costs, and Shadows revives from hand, face-up Extra Deck, or GY.

Medius Reopens The Engine

Medius can add or Special Summon a Power Patron monster from Deck on summon. Its GY effect can put a monster back to Special Summon itself, giving a follow-up Normal/Special body.

If a route gets interrupted, check what was added face-up to the Extra Deck before ending the turn. A destroyed Pendulum body may already have replaced itself with the next starter, search, or recovery piece.

Latest Tournament Variants

Recent topping lists show two main TCG branches: a pure Pendulum shell with Amorphage and high staple density, and an Artmage hybrid that converts Medius into fusion pressure. OCG results also show Astrograph/Electrumite style builds, which require legality checks before copying into TCG.

Side Deck Options And Counters

Side choices below are pulled from recent topping lists and mapped to the likely June 2026 field around Dracotail, Branded, Kewl Tune, DoomZ, Artmage, Maliss, Sky Striker, Radiant Typhoon, and mirror/Pendulum decks.

Going First Locks

Dominus, Solemn cards, and D Barrier protect a moderate board and punish high-commitment combo hands. Mistaken Arrest appears in topping sides when search chains matter.

Going Second Breakers

Sphere Mode and Lava Golem answer towers-style fields. Evenly, Dark Ruler, Book of Eclipse, Droplet, Lightning Storm, and Duster are the broad board-breaker suite.

Hand Trap Suite

Current lists lean on Mulcharmy cards, Droll, Ash, Belle, Ogre, Nibiru, and Bystials. Use Droll after the first add; use Ogre or removal on scales when it strands the hand.

Kewl Tune / Synchro

Plan: Droll after first search, Nibiru before the protected end state, D Barrier calling Synchro when legal, Book/Dark Ruler to turn off layered boards.

Branded / Dracotail

Plan: D Barrier Fusion, Ash key fusion access, Belle/Bystials on GY extension, Super Poly into Garura/Mudragon/Dragostapelia when board states allow.

Maliss / Cyberse Link

Plan: D Barrier does not call Link, so favor Droll, Fuwalos, Nibiru, Bystials, Sphere Mode, and Dark Ruler. Do not rely on one negate.

Mirror / Pendulum

Plan: remove scales, stop Terminus/Medius, force Droll early, and make Jupredo/Nervedo triggers awkward. Evenly and Lightning punish overbuilt boards.

Control / Backrow

Plan: Duster, Lightning, Heavy Storm where legal, Solemn suite, and restrained commitment. Keep enough follow-up in face-up Extra Deck for the second push.
