Pendulum toolbox combo — checked June 8, 2026

Power Patron

A Pendulum toolbox deck that turns cards placed face-up in the Extra Deck into extension, searches, recursion, and Level 12 boss access. The strongest lines route toward Nerva, Junora, and Jupiter, then cash those types in for Vidolium, Vidolia, Viraia, or Shadows.

Recent evidence: Belgium National Top 4, YCS Columbus Top 16 Role: Pendulum midrange combo Win: triple boss types into Vidolium Risk: Droll and scale removal are high impact

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Power Patron plays like a branching resource graph. A starter places a Pendulum body face-up in the Extra Deck, that body fires its trigger, and the next piece decides whether you extend, search interaction, recover, or move into a boss type.

  • 1Load the face-up Extra Deck. Pendulum Treasure, Shade the Obscure, Terminus, and self-destruction effects turn the Extra Deck into a live hand — the face-up triggers are your real starters.
  • 2Choose the trigger. Nervedo summons a body, Jupredo searches Power Patron/DoomZ Spell/Trap, Junoldo recurs, and Vidolium searches any Power Patron card.
  • 3Convert to boss types. The Level 10 monster effects destroy themselves and summon the matching boss from the Extra Deck: Nerva (Fusion), Junora (Synchro), Jupiter (Xyz).
  • 4Cash the payoff. Triple-type boards unlock the deck's strongest text: Vidolia GY lock, Viraia protected monster activations, Shadows trap banishes, and Vidolium as a 5000 ATK closer.

Boss Payoff Rules And Format Notes

  • 1Nerva needs Artmage monster-effect support for the wipe. Nerva can rewrite an Artmage monster effect into "destroy all cards your opponent controls" — do not count a Pendulum scale effect unless it is a legal monster effect.
  • 2Junora is strongest in the Elfnote hybrid. Pure Power Patron mainly values the on-summon face-up negate; the column material lock scales harder with Elfnote monsters.
  • 3Vidolium is both searcher and finisher. Its face-up Extra Deck trigger searches any Power Patron card, but its summon asks you to return 1 Fusion, 1 Synchro, and 1 Xyz from field/GY.

Shells: pure, Artmage hybrid, OCG Astrograph, and Elfnote-adjacent. Map side choices to the likely June 2026 field around Dracotail, Branded, Kewl Tune, DoomZ, Artmage, Maliss, Sky Striker, Radiant Typhoon, and mirror/Pendulum decks.

Artmage hybrid

Medius bridges into Artmage Power Patron, which quick-fuses into Artmage Fusions or Nerva and replaces itself with an Artmage Spell/Trap from GY. Note it locks Extra Deck summons to Fusions while its restriction applies — sequence Pendulum/link access first.

Going second break

Pure lists often main Super Polymerization and board breakers. Clear floodgates or negates first, then Pendulum Summon into link or boss pressure.

Interrupted-route recovery

If a route gets stopped, check what was added face-up to the Extra Deck before ending the turn — a destroyed body may already have replaced itself with the next starter, search, or recovery piece.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Protect the first real search. Hands that expose multiple searches should plan around Droll & Lock Bird. If Nervedo can summon the missing body without adding, take that route before loading extra adds.
  • 2Do not spend Vidolium too early. It is the high scale in many pure hands and also the broadest Power Patron search — destroy it only when scale math and the next search are covered.
  • 3Use Terminus as both starter and threat. It sends a Power Patron from Deck/Extra Deck and can add Medius; in GY it converts battle into a face-down banish.
  • 4Jupredo is interaction access. Face-up it searches the Power Patron/DoomZ Spell/Trap suite; in scale it can trade itself plus a Power Patron/DoomZ body for a field pop.
  • 5Decide which trigger to spend before you destroy or place a Pendulum card face-up — sequencing matters more than memorizing one line.

Recovery Options

  • 1Face-up Extra Deck becomes hand. Junoldo recovers a Power Patron or Elfnote card from GY or face-up Extra Deck, Nervedo turns face-up Extra Deck into another body, Vidolium searches the missing piece.
  • 2Graveyard lines stay relevant. Terminus in GY threatens battle banish, Viraia in GY replaces future top-deck banish costs, and Shadows revives from hand, face-up Extra Deck, or GY.
  • 3Medius reopens the engine. It adds or Special Summons a Power Patron monster from Deck on summon, and its GY effect can put a monster back to Special Summon itself for a follow-up body.

What Stops You

  • 1Droll & Lock Bird after the first add can strand a search-heavy hand — it is the single highest-impact card against you.
  • 2Scale removal (Ghost Ogre and targeted backrow/scale pops) when it breaks the Pendulum Summon math or strands the hand.
  • 3Nibiru into greedy lines, and board breakers into ceiling boards that spent every face-up Extra Deck trigger.
  • 4Artmage Power Patron's own Fusion lock if you sequence it before needed Pendulum/link plays.

How You Counter Back

  • 1Current lists lean on the Mulcharmy cards, Droll, Ash, Belle, Ogre, Nibiru, and Bystials. Use Droll after the opponent's first add; use Ogre or removal on scales when it strands their hand.
  • 2Destroyed Pendulum bodies replace themselves face-up in the Extra Deck, so trading one-for-one with your engine pieces is often fine — bank the trigger and keep follow-up.
  • 3One boss plus set Dominus Impulse/Solemn Accusation outlasts most board-breaker hands.
