Shaddoll Cheatsheet

Deck map

Playstyle and Win Condition

control grind
Trigger by effect

Every Fusion material choice should either add a card, remove a card, or set up the next turn.

Starters

Fusion spells, Mathematician, Shaddoll Fusion into an opponent Extra Deck monster, Dracotail Lukias, Ketu Dracotail.

Send targets

Squamata extends sends, Beast converts to cards, Hedgehog searches, Dragon removes backrow, Wendi places bodies, Ariel manages GY.

Fusion outputs

Construct for sends and battle pressure, Winda for Special Summon cap, Apkallone for negate/search, Meshahrail and Wendikurhu for newer pure lines.

Closing

Trade up with Schism, force awkward turns under Winda, then recycle Fusion spells and attack over simplified boards.

Meta posture

Pure Shaddoll is more rogue/control. The 2026 tournament result came from Dracotail Shaddoll, which uses Shaddolls as value material.

Weak points

GY hate, Fusion negation, banishing Schism material, and removal that outs Winda without Special Summoning.

Why this shell topped

Edwin Martin Strom IV's Shaddoll Dracotail list reached Top 4 at Genesys Proto-YCS Richmond on February 14, 2026. Konami's Round 10 top-table breakdown also showed Shaddoll Dracotail and Shaddoll Magistus Dracotail represented among the leading tables.

Dracotail = starter density Shaddoll = material payoff Kaiju + Dark Ruler = going-second plan Side Shaddoll Fusion = punish ED boards

The Richmond list is useful as a structure map, not as a copy-paste June 2026 TCG list. The current official list has since hit multiple Dracotail cards.

Current build notes

  • Keep the Shaddoll package lean unless you are building pure around the new Terminal World 3 support.
  • Choose Shaddoll Beast when you need raw cards, Squamata when you need access, Dragon when backrow matters, and Ariel when GY interaction matters.

Cheatsheet notes

Tips

Sequencing

  • Send Squamata first only if the extra send is worth more than a direct Beast draw, Hedgehog search, or Dragon removal.
  • When Construct sends Schism, have a way to send Construct to GY so the recovery trigger can add Schism back.
  • Use El Shaddoll Fusion after the opponent commits targeting removal or battle math.

Winda discipline

  • Winda affects both players. Do not lock yourself before your own necessary Special Summons.
  • Winda plus no follow-up loses to normal summon pressure. Pair it with Schism, disruption, or real card advantage.
  • Schism into Winda is often better than making Winda early and passing.

Meta adjustments

  • Into combo, prioritize Winda timing, Droll-style search denial, and board breakers that do not require Special Summons.
  • Into GY decks, Ariel and D.D. Crow are interaction, but heavy floodgate GY hate can also turn off your own engine.
  • Into backrow, Dragon, Cosmic Cyclone, and Super Polymerization targets matter more than raw Winda.

After disruption

Recovery Options

Fusion sent to GY

Construct Apkallone

Recover Shaddoll Fusion, El Shaddoll Fusion, or Schism. Apkallone searches then discards, which can trigger another Shaddoll if discarded by effect.

GY toolbox

Ariel Falco

Ariel recycles or attacks opposing GY lines. Falco sets itself and can turn into material next turn.

Trap recursion

Incarnation Core

Incarnation flips your set Shaddoll for value or revives by banishing with a Shaddoll. Core can recover a Shaddoll spell/trap when sent.

Hybrid grind

Arthalion Horn

Combo boards

Dark Ruler Droplet Gameciel

Break the board without using your Normal Summon, then land Shaddoll Fusion or Schism pressure.

Backrow and trap decks

Cosmic Dragon Duster

Dragon is searchable and triggerable by Fusion; Cosmic is best into Schism mirrors and field-spell decks.

Search-heavy decks

Droll Mistaken Arrest Ash

Do not overside cards that also stop your own Hedgehog, Void Liberation, or Dracotail search routes unless the matchup demands it.

GY decks

D.D. Crow Ghost Belle Ariel

Prefer pinpoint GY interaction over blanket hate when your own follow-up needs Fusion material in GY.

Extra Deck decks

Super Poly Garura Mudragon

Shaddoll Fusion punishes an established Extra Deck monster. Super Polymerization converts their board before your engine starts.

What counters Shaddoll

Called By Imperm Evenly

Common opposing plans: stop Construct/Apkallone, banish Schism materials, remove Winda without Special Summoning, or trade one card for your set Schism.

Side pattern by role

  • Going first: add Dimensional Barrier, Anti-Spell Fragrance, or extra trap disruption only when they do not conflict with your own spell-heavy hand.
  • Going second: add Kaijus, Dark Ruler, Super Polymerization, Ultimate Slayer, and Cosmic based on the board you expect.
  • Mirror and Fusion decks: Cosmic on Schism, Ariel on GY material, and Super Polymerization targets matter.

Counterplay against Winda

  • Normal Summon pressure can force Winda trades if the Shaddoll player lacks Schism follow-up.
  • Kaiju, Droplet, Dark Ruler, Imperm, Book-style effects, or battle removal can break the lock without building a chain of Special Summons.
  • If Schism is telegraphed, banish a needed material or remove Schism before the opponent can pick the correct Attribute Fusion.
