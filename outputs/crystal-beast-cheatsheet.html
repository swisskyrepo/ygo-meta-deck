Crystal Beast

Top-to-bottom route map for Conclave Control, the Ruby OTK, Overdrive pushes, and the current combo-lab variants. Built from MDM combo/counter records, NESHY's guide notes, recent 2026 meta deck listings, and the latest located Crystal Beast TCG tournament top.

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Crystal Beast wins by turning monsters into persistent Spell/Trap Zone resources, then converting those crystals into interruption, extra bodies, or lethal pressure. Sapphire Pegasus is the cleanest one-card route into placements, Rank 4s, and Rainbow Bridge of the Heart triggers.

  • 1Conclave Control: stick Heart, keep a Crystal Beast in the backrow, and use Crystal Conclave plus Crystal Miracle as low-commitment interaction.
  • 2Ruby OTK: use Golden Rule or Awakening of the Crystal Ultimates to put Ruby Carbuncle on field while multiple crystals are loaded, then flood the board and attack.
  • 3Overdrive finish: after seven different names are assembled, convert an Ultimate Crystal monster into Ultimate Crystal Rainbow Dragon Overdrive for an 11,000 ATK body and a board-shuffle Quick Effect.

Key Pairs And Format Notes

  • 1Heart + Crystal Bond: activate Heart first so Bond placing Zenith can trigger Heart's bounce.
  • 2Golden Rule + Ruby: Golden Rule places two crystals and summons a different Crystal Beast from hand or GY; Ruby turns loaded backrow into bodies.
  • 3Foolish Burial Goods + Salvation: FBG sends Rainbow Bridge of Salvation, which later searches a Crystal Beast plus a Field Spell (Necrovalley, Rainbow Ruins, Duel Academy, Kyoutou Waterfront).
  • 4Awakening + Battle Phase: summon Ruby in Battle Phase to dodge Main Phase only cards such as Effect Veiler and Nibiru.

Status: rogue. No MDM spreadsheet entry exists for the deck, and the current June 2026 pressure decks are Kewl Tune, Branded Dracotail, DoomZ, and Elfnote.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Heart before Bond: if Heart is face-up before Bond places Zenith, Heart can target an opponent's card and bounce itself plus that card before you commit a summon.
  • 2Respect "when": Pegasus and Ruby can miss timing. If Awakening summons Pegasus on the opponent's turn, use Awakening as Chain Link 1 so Pegasus can trigger.
  • 3Flip Conclave early: Conclave must already be face-up for its bounce effect. Flip it in Draw Phase when you need interaction, unless hiding information matters more.
  • 4Double Conclave trick: two Conclaves can target the same Crystal Beast and different opponent cards; resolution returns as much as possible, producing a multi-card bounce.
  • 5Battle Phase Ruby: Awakening can summon Ruby in Battle Phase, avoiding Effect Veiler and Nibiru.
  • 6Zone management: do not set extra backrow casually — Pegasus, Bond, Heart, Golden Rule, Conclave, and Miracle all need open S/T zones to stay live.

Recovery Options

  • 1After destruction: most Crystal Beast monsters can place themselves face-up in the S/T Zone instead of going to GY, preserving material for Heart, Conclave, Ruby, and Miracle.
  • 2After losing Heart: Heart can bounce itself with its own trigger, so replay it later. Rainbow Bridge finds it again, and Salvation can shift into a Field Spell plan.
  • 3After hand traps: if Pegasus or Bond is stopped, fall back to Heart plus set Conclave/Miracle, or make Bagooska/Abyss Dweller with spare Level 4 bodies.
  • 4After a board wipe: use Miracle's GY effect after a Crystal Beast is placed, Salvation's GY search, and Ruby from GY/hand through Golden Rule or Awakening to rebuild bodies.

What Stops You

  • 1Ash on Bond, Rainbow Bridge, Zenith, or the Salvation search; Imperm/Veiler on Pegasus; Droll after the first add.
  • 2Banish or bounce effects (Cosmic, Ghost Ogre, Evenly, Feather Duster) on Heart, Conclave, and loaded crystals — destroying Crystal Beast monsters often just feeds the S/T Zone.
  • 3Nibiru before the Battle Phase Ruby window, Dominus Impulse on summon effects, and Droll/Fuwalos/Maxx "C" against the MD-style combo routes.

How You Counter Back

  • 1Destruction becomes crystals: most Crystal Beasts place themselves in the S/T Zone instead of dying, so removal-heavy decks feed your Ruby and Conclave material.
  • 2Heart self-bounces to dodge removal, and Awakening's Battle Phase Ruby dodges Main Phase only interruption.
  • 3When hand-trapped, downgrade to the floor: Heart plus set Conclave/Miracle, or Bagooska/Abyss Dweller from spare Level 4s, instead of all-in Ruby exposure.
