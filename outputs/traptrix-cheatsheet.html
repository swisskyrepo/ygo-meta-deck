i Playstyle and win condition

Put Sera online, then make every trap trade up.

1. First priority

Link a non-Link Traptrix into Traptrix Sera. Sera converts Normal Trap activations into deck summons, then converts Traptrix monster effects into set Hole traps.

2. Control texture

Rafflesia turns the deck into a toolbox. Send Gravedigger for hand/GY effects, Floodgate to lock a summon face-down, or Trap Hole Nightmare for a special-summoned monster effect.

3. Closing

After two or three trades, use Pinguicula searches, Holeutea revival, Allomerus bodies, or Atypus boost/negation to turn resource advantage into lethal pressure.

i Tips and counters

Meta tips that matter most

Do not autopilot into Sera hand traps

The current MDM Traptrix combo note warns that adding or setting Holeutea can be wrong if a response window exposes Ash/Veiler/Imperm pressure on Sera. When the opponent can stop Sera, it is often better to set real traps and pass with fewer summons.

Rafflesia changes the Nibiru math

The Myrmeleo + Holeutea route reaches Rafflesia as the fifth summon. Once Rafflesia is live, it can copy Gravedigger's Trap Hole to answer Nibiru and other hand/GY monster effects.

Board breakers are the real fear

MDM counter records rate Super Polymerization, Evenly Matched, and Red Reboot as high-impact answers. Red Reboot is forbidden in the current TCG Advanced list, but it remains relevant to format-specific or Master Duel counter notes.

Do not give away free trap triggers

Dominus Impulse, Spiral, and Spark were marked as backfire counters into Traptrix because once Sera is live, Normal Traps can trigger its engine. Traptrix lists can still play selected Dominus traps, but you must respect attribute locks and timing.

+ Recovery options

Winning after the first wave

Holeutea in GY

Banish Holeutea to revive Myrmeleo for backrow removal, Pudica for a temporary monster banish, Dionaea to reset a Hole, or a properly summoned Sera if you need the engine back.

Cularia grind line

If you can afford a Link 2, Cularia lets Hole traps reset instead of going to GY and revives a Traptrix during the End Phase. It is slower than Rafflesia, but excellent when the duel becomes resource based.

Atypus close

With a Normal Trap in GY, Atypus gives Traptrix monsters a 1000 ATK push, negates face-up cards up to your Insect/Plant count, and can banish a Normal Trap to destroy one.

i Side deck options

Matchup cards and counter-plans

Use the Oklahoma Top 8 side as the baseline: Retaliating "C", Mulcharmy Purulia, Ash, Droll, Duster, Lightning Storm, Triple Tactics Talent, Evenly, Iron Thunder, and Solemn Judgment.

Card legality note

Red Reboot is a strong theoretical trap-deck counter in MDM records, but the official TCG list effective May 18, 2026 has Red Reboot forbidden. Keep the side deck format-specific.
