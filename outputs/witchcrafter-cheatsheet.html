Playstyle And Win Condition

Witchcrafter converts spells into costs, then reclaims Witchcrafter spells in the End Phase. The deck wins by surviving the first exchange, rebuilding every turn, and turning a stocked hand into disruption and lethal pressure.

i Core Loop
+ +

Small Witchcrafters tribute themselves and discard a Spell to summon a boss from Deck. Schmietta then loads the GY with the exact Witchcrafter card you need next.

i Control Bosses
+ +

Verre blankets opposing field monster effects, Haine picks off face-up cards, and Vice-Madame turns Spell or Spellcaster effects into add, summon, or destroy.

i 2026 Upgrade
+ +

Walpurgis changes costs into deck sends, Terracottan fuses, and Madame Rilliona floods multiple differently attributed Witchcrafters after Fusion Summon.

i Closing
+ +

Once the opponent is low on resources, reveal multiple Spells with Verre for a huge battle boost, then use Collaboration or repeated Haine/Verre bodies to end the game.

Tips

Most games are decided by sequencing. Keep a Spell available for costs, avoid spending duplicate Witchcrafter spell names too early, and know which resource you are supposed to recover in the End Phase.

i Walpurgis Replacement

When a Witchcrafter monster would discard for cost, Walpurgis can send a Witchcrafter Spell/Trap from Deck instead. Send Celebration for fusion routes, Holiday for rebuilds, or Patronus/Masterpiece for grind.

i Vice-Madame Chains

Vice-Madame triggers from a Spell Card/effect or a non-Fusion Spellcaster monster effect. Choose add when low on gas, summon when pushing, and destroy when the opponent commits a must-answer card.

i End Phase Discipline

Each Witchcrafter spell can only use one of its listed effects that turn. If you activate Creation, that same copy cannot recover itself in the End Phase. Track which names are spent.

i Hand Trap Texture

Current combo notes rate simple negation lower than field-spell removal. Ash can slow you, but Ghost Ogre or removal on Walpurgis can hurt more if your hand needed its replacement effect.

i Verre Timing

Verre negates opposing monsters currently on field when her quick effect resolves. Use it before battle math or before the opponent converts those bodies into Extra Deck material.

i Into Mulcharmy

Use Quick-Play and Quick Effect summons where possible to make the opponent draw at awkward times or reduce extra draws. The deck can pass on a smaller board if the hand recurs enough spells.

Recovery Options

Witchcrafter is strongest when the first board trades evenly. These are the rebuild buttons to preserve, mill, or search before committing to a second push.

GY And Banished Resource Loop

+ +
  • Patronus recycles a banished or GY Spellcaster into Deck, then searches a Witchcrafter Spell/Trap.
  • Genni copies a Witchcrafter spell from GY while dodging Spell/Trap negates because the copied effect is applied by a monster effect.
  • Schmietta sends whatever the current hand is missing: Holiday to revive, Masterpiece for a later Deck summon, or Patronus for long game.

Board Rebuild

+ +
  • Holiday revives Verre or Haine after the opponent spends removal.
  • Bystreet prevents the first destruction of each Witchcrafter monster each turn and can replace a discard from field.
  • Madame Rilliona can revive herself in the End Phase from GY by revealing a Spell, forcing opponents to fully answer the Fusion line.
  • Celebration returns in End Phase if it reached GY and you control a Witchcrafter monster, giving follow-up fusion or removal fodder.

Best Counters Against Witchcrafter

  • Remove or negate Walpurgis before the discard-replacement line resolves.
  • Use D.D. Crow or Ghost Belle on Holiday, Patronus, Genni's copied spell setup, or Celebration materials.
  • Force Vice-Madame early, then negate it when the Witchcrafter player has no follow-up Spellcaster effect.
  • Keep Spell removal for Bystreet or Secret Village instead of firing it at low-impact set cards.

Playing Through Counters

  • If you already have access to a small Witchcrafter, hold Walpurgis search until the opponent commits removal.
  • Use Genni to copy Creation or Holiday when Spell/Trap negates are likely.
  • Summon Verre first against monster-heavy boards; summon Haine first when you need immediate card removal or targeting protection.
  • Accept a smaller board when facing Mulcharmy. A Verre plus recovered spells can be better than giving the opponent several draws.
