+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Witchcrafter converts Spells into costs, then reclaims its Witchcrafter Spells in the End Phase. The deck wins by surviving the first exchange, rebuilding every turn, and turning a stocked hand into disruption and lethal pressure.

  • 1Core loop: small Witchcrafters (Schmietta, Pittore, Genni) tribute themselves and discard a Spell to summon a boss from Deck. Schmietta then loads the GY with the exact Witchcrafter card you need next.
  • 2Control bosses: Madame Verre blankets opposing field monster effects, Haine picks off face-up cards, and Vice-Madame turns Spell or Spellcaster effects into add, summon, or destroy.
  • 32026 upgrade: Walpurgis changes costs into deck sends, Terracottan fuses, and Madame Rilliona floods multiple differently attributed Witchcrafters after Fusion Summon.
  • 4Closing: once the opponent is low on resources, reveal multiple Spells with Verre for a huge battle boost, then use Collaboration or repeated Haine/Verre bodies to end the game.

Win Condition / Format Notes

  • 1Starter: Creation is the primary modern 1-card route, reaching the full Fusion board through Verre, the Maid of Endymion and Walpurgis.
  • 2Grind: every unused Witchcrafter Spell returns from the GY in the End Phase while you control a Witchcrafter monster — the hand refills faster than most decks can out-trade.
  • 3Ceiling: high-roll OCG builds stack Distortion omni-negates and Pupils copies on top of Verre + Haine. Treat that as ceiling planning, not a guaranteed pure-list baseline.
  • 4Format: the newest indexed tournament top under the Witchcrafter tag is a hybrid — Genni plus Verre Magic - Lacrima of Light inside Magistus Fairy Tail — not pure Witchcrafter.

Small Witchcrafter fallback

When the hand is light or you are playing under Mulcharmy: Normal a small Witchcrafter, discard a Spell, summon the boss the matchup needs, and lean on End Phase recovery.

Genni anti-negate line

Against Spell/Trap negation, banish Genni to copy Creation or Holiday from GY — the copied effect is applied by a monster effect and dodges S/T negates.

Haine pressure branch

Best when a single face-up card must die or when targeting protection matters; Bystreet shields the first destruction of each Witchcrafter monster each turn.

OCG max ceiling

If the hand already reaches Rilliona, Pupils and Distortion become the high-ceiling interaction package; some OCG lists add a Dragoon-style negate on top.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Walpurgis replacement: when a Witchcrafter monster would discard for cost, Walpurgis can send a Witchcrafter Spell/Trap from Deck instead. Send Celebration for Fusion routes, Holiday for rebuilds, Patronus/Masterpiece for grind.
  • 2Vice-Madame chains: Vice-Madame triggers from a Spell Card/effect or a non-Fusion Spellcaster monster effect. Choose add when low on gas, summon when pushing, destroy when the opponent commits a must-answer card.
  • 3End Phase discipline: each Witchcrafter Spell can only use one of its listed effects that turn. If you activate Creation, that copy cannot recover itself in the End Phase — track which names are spent.
  • 4Hand trap texture: current combo notes rate simple negation lower than field-spell removal. Ash Blossom can slow you, but Ghost Ogre or removal on Walpurgis hurts more if your hand needed its replacement effect.
  • 5Verre timing: Verre negates opposing monsters currently on field when her Quick Effect resolves. Use it before battle math or before those bodies become Extra Deck material.
  • 6Into Mulcharmy: use Quick-Play and Quick Effect summons to make Purulia/Fuwalos draws awkward. The deck can pass on a smaller board if the hand recurs enough Spells.

Recovery Options

  • 1Patronus recycles a banished or GY Spellcaster into Deck, then searches a Witchcrafter Spell/Trap — the cleanest way to undo your own Genni/Schmietta banish costs.
  • 2Holiday revives Verre or Haine after the opponent spends removal; Bystreet prevents the first destruction of each Witchcrafter monster each turn and can replace a discard from field.
  • 3Madame Rilliona can revive herself in the End Phase from GY by revealing a Spell, forcing opponents to fully answer the Fusion line; Celebration returns in the End Phase if it reached GY while you control a Witchcrafter monster.
  • 4Potterie can recover a Witchcrafter card when your hand is empty — the emergency grind name. Schmietta sends whatever the current hand is missing.

+ Side Deck Options And Counters

Side plans should respect your own GY dependence. Do not side floodgates that strand your Witchcrafter Spell recovery unless the matchup is worth the cost.
Matchup / problemSide optionsHow to use them
Combo swarm — many summons, fragile search chains
Nibiru and Droll buy the turn your engine wants; Book of Eclipse forces interaction without spending your Normal Summon. Nibiru can conflict with your own high-summon ceiling — keep it for going-second plans.
Established boards — negates plus untargetable bodies
Super Polymerization turns spare Spells into live pressure; Ultimate Slayer clears while loading Extra Deck utility. Super Poly and Droplet both ask for discard material — repeated hand costs reduce Verre's ceiling.
Backrow / trap — floodgates, removal, trap engines
Duster/Cosmic Cyclone clear anti-spell or graveyard hate; Evenly Matched resets boards before your Normal Summon; Judgment protects a setup board going first. Evenly conflicts with Battle Phase lethal — decide reset or kill before committing.
GY decks — recursive monsters, graveyard chains
Crow/Belle interact without turning off your own engine. Dimensional Fissure appears in the Magistus Fairy Tail side plan and can steal games, but it is hostile to pure Witchcrafter recovery — use it mainly in hybrid plans that win before the grind matters.
Spell-heavy decks — power spells, board breakers
Secret Village is live because your board is Spellcaster-heavy; Barrier and Solemns cover Extra Deck or monster-effect blowouts while Distortion becomes searchable archetype interaction. Village fails if your only Spellcaster is removed — protect with Bystreet, Judgment, or a second body.

What Stops You

  • 1Removal or negation on Walpurgis before the discard-replacement line resolves.
  • 2D.D. Crow or Ghost Belle on Holiday, Patronus, Genni's copied-spell setup, or Celebration materials.
  • 3Forcing Vice-Madame early, then negating it when there is no follow-up Spellcaster effect.
  • 4Spell removal saved for Bystreet or Secret Village instead of low-impact set cards.

How You Counter Back

  • 1If you already have a small Witchcrafter, hold the Walpurgis search until the opponent commits removal.
  • 2Use Genni to copy Creation or Holiday when Spell/Trap negates are likely.
  • 3Summon Verre first against monster-heavy boards; summon Haine first when you need immediate removal or targeting protection.
  • 4Accept a smaller board into Mulcharmy. A Verre plus recovered Spells can beat giving the opponent several draws.
