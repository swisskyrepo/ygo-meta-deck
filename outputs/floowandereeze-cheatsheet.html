i Playstyle and win condition

Win by turning both Main Phases into your turn.

Floowandereeze spends Normal Summons as a resource. The small birds replace themselves, banish themselves when leaving the field, and return to hand whenever another Winged Beast is Normal Summoned.

Primary loop

  • Robina finds Eglen; Eglen finds a big Winged Beast.
  • Empen searches Dreaming Town first unless your hand already covers the opponent turn.
  • Dreaming Town converts the opponent Main Phase into Robina -> Eglen -> Raiza/Apex/Empen.

Win condition

  • Empen blanks activated effects from Attack Position Special Summoned monsters.
  • Dreaming Town in the GY flips the opponent's field face-down after your Tribute Summon.
  • Raiza stacks threats; Apex Avian covers the one card that matters.

Current caveat

TCG legality changed after older Floowandereeze tops. As of the May 18, 2026 TCG list, Dimension Shifter is Limited, while Harpie's Feather Storm, Dimensional Barrier, Fossil Dyna, and Barrier Statue of the Torrent are Forbidden. Master Duel and local formats can differ.

+ Latest tournament-top variant found

YCS Guayaquil Top 32: Greed/Mist Valley Floowandereeze.

The latest public TCG tournament top I found is Jean Carlos Flor's YCS Guayaquil Top 32 list from November 2, 2024. The event had 330 players and two Floowandereeze decks in Top 32. Counts below are historical and should be adjusted for the current list.

Variant takeaways

  • 3 Map, 3 Advent, 3 Duality, 2 Extravagance, 1 Prosperity built for high starter density.
  • 2 Toccan and 1 Stri leaned into banished-card recycling more than pure minimal engines.
  • Thrust/Talent and Evenly/Dark Ruler gave the main deck live going-second cards.
  • Side cards included Swift Scarecrow, Droll, Dimensional Fissure, Lightning Storm, and extra Harpie's Feather Storm under that older format.
