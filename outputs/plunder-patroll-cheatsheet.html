Plunder Patroll

A top-to-bottom cheatsheet for routing Plunder Patroll hands into Jord, Blackbeard, and the right ship for the matchup. Practical branches over rote memorization.

Meta lens: TCG June 2026 side choices Role: reactive control with a combo spine Win: Lys negate + Blackbeard reload Latest tournament top found: May 12, 2024

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Plunder Patroll is a reactive control deck with a combo spine. It asks what Attributes the opponent has on field or in GY, then turns any Effect Monster into the matching ship. Core rule: tag effects care about a monster Attribute your opponent controls or has in GY — Booty, Jord tokens, Kaijus, and Adventure Token lines are valuable because they create the Attributes your ships need.

  • 1First objective: reach Jord or Blackbeard with a Plunder in rotation. Jord fixes Attributes with tokens; Blackbeard converts any Effect Monster into the best ship and draws.
  • 2Primary lock point: end on Blackbeard plus Lys when possible. Lys handles monster effects, Moerk banishes monsters, and Brann banishes backrow while adding more engine.
  • 3How games end: snowball equips, Shipyarrrd ATK boosts, and repeated draws. When the board stalls, Moerk into Zeus or Brann-boosted damage closes games.

Ship Jobs And Format Notes

Choose ships by job, not by habit: Lys is monster-effect insurance, Moerk answers face-up effect monsters and protects from destruction, Brann hits backrow and pushes damage, Jord rebuilds and fixes Attributes.

As of June 2026, plan side cards for Kewl Tune, Branded/Dracotail, Yummy, Maliss, DoomZ, Sky Striker, and Power Patron style boards. The latest Plunder tournament top found is May 12, 2024; newer 2026 lists exist as community decks rather than tournament tops.

If Ash hits Shipyarrrd

Pivot to a Normal Summon plus extender line: Whitebeard or Goldenhair into Bluebeard or Redbeard. Do not burn Goldenhair if it is your only extender.

If Imperm hits Jord

Use remaining bodies to reach Blackbeard anyway, or set Booty / Emblem for the opponent turn instead of forcing the full line.

Attribute fixing

If the Attribute your ship needs is missing, create it before tagging: Booty changes an Attribute and revives, Jord makes LIGHT tokens, and Kaijus like Gameciel or Radian give the opponent a WATER or DARK body while removing a boss.

Chimera high ceiling

With discard fuel already in hand, the Gazelle route adds Chimera King plus Berfomet King follow-up and possible hand pressure on top of the Lys board. If Gazelle gets stopped, do not chase the full line — convert remaining Fiend bodies into Blackbeard.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Sequence discard extenders before committing the Normal Summon: Yarrrd + Gold or Rite + Gold lines start without it, preserving a fallback if Ash or Imperm trades with your first effect.
  • 2If Shipyarrrd resolves, use the searched Plunder as discard fuel. If it eats Ash, you may still have Whitebeard, Gold, Blue, or Adventure to continue.
  • 3Do not tag blindly into an empty Attribute map: check the opponent GY before committing Blackbeard. If the needed Attribute is missing, create it with Booty, Jord tokens, or a Kaiju first.
  • 4Use Lys to convert equip value into cards: Lys only searches after the negate if equipped with a Plunder card — Redbeard, Emblem, Shipping, and Parrrty make that live more often.
  • 5Respect the locks: Whitebeard, Goldenhair, and Blackeyes can lock you into Plunders for the rest of the turn — make generic Extra Deck plays first if needed.
  • 6Choose ships by job, not by habit; and watch June 2026 pressure points (Droll, Imperm, Lancea, Cosmic Cyclone) when planning your turn.

Recovery Options

  • 1GY to hand/body: Blackeyes adds a Plunder from GY while summoning itself, Goldenhair turns any discard into a body, and Whitebeard sent to GY refills from Deck.
  • 2Spell/Trap recursion: Shipyarrrd sets itself from GY by returning a Plunder S/T zone card, Shipping equips from Deck, and Parrrty re-equips once per Duel.
  • 3Board rebuild after a wipe: Moerk can protect Plunder cards by detaching, Jord returns a Plunder card from GY and becomes a scale, and Booty revives while changing Attributes.
  • 4Runick or Adventure rebuild: Runick Fountain reloads spells and Hugin finds it; Adventure provides a token, Fateful search lines, and Gryphon protection when legal in your format.
  • 5The deck wins long games by making the same cards matter twice: equip, revive, add back, reset, then tag again.

+ Side Deck Options And Counters

Matchup / problemSide optionsHow to use them
Kewl Tune / combo search chains
Droll is strongest when they need multiple adds to sculpt hand and top deck. Nibiru is fine only if your own token plan still leaves a path to Blackbeard. Droplet converts dead Plunder cards into board access going second.
Branded / Dracotail Fusion piles
Dimensional Barrier calling Fusion buys the cleanest turn if you can protect it. Ghost Belle and D.D. Crow hit GY fusion or recursion points. Super Poly is live into boards that share Type/Attribute lanes.
Maliss / banish-heavy lines
Lancea is a turn-skip style card against banish-dependent turns. Cosmic is better than destruction against cards that replace themselves. Hold Blackbeard until an Attribute exists in GY if they leave the field empty.
Sky Striker / backrow control
Brann threatens banish plus monster search if equipped. Cosmic on recursion points beats firing at replaceable spells. Shipyarrrd damage matters because control mirrors become low-resource games.
Power Patron / Pendulum boards
Dimensional Barrier calling Pendulum can stop the explosive turn. Book of Eclipse and Evenly Matched trade through layered monster interaction. Kaijus force Attributes and answer hard-to-target bosses.
Rogue and locals
The Kaiju package is unusually good here because it removes a monster and turns on ships. Talent and Thrust punish hand traps aimed at Shipyarrrd, Jord, or Blackbeard. Do not over-side out Plunder names; the ships need discard fuel.

What Stops You

  • 1Ash Blossom on Shipyarrrd or Droll against the search chain — the deck's first effect is its biggest choke point.
  • 2Infinite Impermanence or Effect Veiler on Jord before the scale and token line resolves.
  • 3Opponents who keep field and GY free of useful Attributes, leaving Blackbeard with no legal tag.
  • 4In Adventure builds, Fateful Adventure is an obvious extra choke point on top of Jord.

How You Counter Back

  • 1Start without the Normal Summon (Yarrrd or Rite lines) so a single hand trap does not end the turn; keep Goldenhair as the held extender.
  • 2Order Whitebeard and Bluebeard triggers to chain-block Ash onto the less important effect.
  • 3Create your own Attributes with Booty, Jord tokens, or Kaijus instead of waiting for the opponent to provide them.
  • 4Triple Tactics Talent and Thrust turn hand traps into card advantage; engine recursion wins most grind games after that.
