Sky Striker Mitsurugi

A top-down cheatsheet for the Striker control shell plus the Mitsurugi Ritual package: how to open, where the lines branch, what the End Phase should look like, and how to side into the June 2026 field.

Last checked: 2026-06-07 UTC Role: midrange control Win: trade, loop, then Linkage or Murakumo push TCG Advanced: May 18, 2026 list

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Sky Striker gives the deck a low-commitment spell engine: trade with Widow Anchor, Shark Cannon, Linkage, Droplet, and Engage recursion while keeping Raye live. Mitsurugi raises the ceiling by turning Habakiri, Pre-Prep, or Prayers into Ritual pressure and tribute-trigger follow-up.

  • 1Use Striker spells while your Main Monster Zones are empty.
  • 2Only put Mitsurugi Rituals in the Main Monster Zone when the interaction is worth turning off Striker spells.
  • 3Win by exhausting responses, then convert with Linkage damage, Murakumo wipe, stolen monsters, or Extra Deck cleanup.

Current Format Assumptions

As of the official May 18, 2026 TCG list, Habakiri is Limited, Droll is Semi-Limited, Hornet Drones is Limited, and King of the Feral Imps is Forbidden. Master Duel lines can differ, so the TCG routes here avoid relying on Feral Imps as the bridge.

Recent exact hybrid tops favored a compact Mitsurugi package inside a heavy spell shell. Radiant Typhoon Sky Striker is the broader post-banlist Striker variant with the most recent YCS Top 4 signal.

Zero bridge

Make Zero only when its added Striker Spell or the Raye/Roze bodies beat Kagari or Shizuku. Tribute it for both Aces from Deck/GY; do not plan on King of the Feral Imps in TCG — it is Forbidden.

Hayate/Kagari value loop

When battle is safe or you need three Spells in GY for the Engage draw: Hayate dumps the needed Spell on a direct attack, Kagari recovers it.

Linkage push (going second)

After trades simplify the board, convert the current Ace into another body with Linkage and count exact damage.

Futsu control (heavier lists, going first)

Ritual Summon Futsu through Ritual or Mirror, set Great Purification, and revive a Reptile when the opponent Special Summons. Tribute a Level 5+ Reptile for the trap at the highest-impact point.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Start with Striker spells when your hand needs Engage value. Start with Pre-Prep when you want to test for Droll before committing Engage.
  • 2After a Ritual monster occupies a Main Monster Zone, plan how it leaves: Link into Camellia/Zeke, tribute with Prayers/Purification, or convert to Dyna Mondo.
  • 3Do not make Zero just because it is available. Kagari or Shizuku is often the tighter line when you need one exact Spell.
  • 4Track three Spells in GY. The Engage draw changes borderline hands into winning hands.
  • 5Against open engine decks, pass on fewer summons with real interaction rather than building a 5+ summon board into Nibiru.

Recovery Options

  • 1Raye recurs after Striker Links leave by battle or opponent effect. Preserve her unless Linkage damage is lethal.
  • 2Kagari gets the best Spell back, usually Engage, Linkage, Widow Anchor, Shark Cannon, or Hornet Drones.
  • 3Kusanagi recovers a Mitsurugi card from GY or banishment, so it fixes Mirror/Ritual attrition and Dimension Shifter games better than most engines.
  • 4Mirror Ritual Summons from hand or GY and can shuffle itself back when your controlled Mitsurugi Ritual is Tributed.
  • 5Dyna Mondo converts Ritual monsters into recursion and gives you a clean Extra Monster Zone body.

i Latest Tournament Variants

Exact hybrid

Compact Mitsurugi Striker

Recent exact hybrid tops often trim Mitsurugi to one Habakiri, one Saji, one Murakumo, three Prayers, one Ritual, and one Mirror. This lowers bricks and leaves room for Striker, Radiant Typhoon, and board breakers.

Heavier engine

Aramasa / Kusanagi / Futsu Build

Heavier lists add Aramasa, Kusanagi, and Futsu to enable fuller Ritual loops, better recovery, and Great Purification boards. The tradeoff is more non-Striker pieces and more Main Monster Zone pressure.

Current Striker trend

Radiant Typhoon Sky Striker

The latest high-profile Striker result found during research was Radiant Typhoon Sky Striker Top 4 at YCS Columbus 2026 on May 23. That supports keeping the Radiant Typhoon spell package in mind even when testing Mitsurugi.

High ceiling

Azamina 60-card Hybrid

A Montevideo WCQ Regional-winning list used a 60-card Azamina/Mitsurugi/Sky Striker shell. It is powerful but much less suitable as a compact cheatsheet baseline because it adds a separate Sinful Spoils/Azamina decision tree.

What Stops You

  • 1Ash on Pre-Prep or Engage denies the highest-value search.
  • 2Droll after Pre-Prep, Engage, or Prayers cuts both engines in half.
  • 3Imperm/Effect Veiler on Saji or Kagari can leave you with bodies but no search.
  • 4Nibiru punishes full hybrid lines after the fifth summon.
  • 5Dimension Shifter hurts Striker GY loops more than Mitsurugi tribute triggers, so lean on Ritual pressure if it resolves.

How You Counter Back

  • 1Bait with a lower-value search when you already have the missing half.
  • 2Use Droplet and Linkage as spell-count builders, not only as board breakers.
  • 3Keep Raye in GY before passing so an opponent's removal can turn into another Ace.
  • 4When ahead, search Widow Anchor/Shark Cannon instead of chasing more combo pieces.
  • 5When behind, prioritize Murakumo, H.A.M.P., Evenly, TY-PHON, or Exciton over pure resource loops.
