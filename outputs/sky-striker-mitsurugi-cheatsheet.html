Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Sky Striker gives the deck a low-commitment spell engine: trade with Widow Anchor, Shark Cannon, Linkage, Droplet, and Engage recursion while keeping Raye live. Mitsurugi raises the ceiling by turning Habakiri, Pre-Prep, or Prayers into Ritual pressure and tribute-trigger follow-up.

  • 1Use Striker spells while your Main Monster Zones are empty.
  • 2Only put Mitsurugi Rituals in the Main Monster Zone when the interaction is worth turning off Striker spells.
  • 3Win by exhausting responses, then convert with Linkage damage, Murakumo wipe, stolen monsters, or Extra Deck cleanup.

Current Format Assumptions

As of the official May 18, 2026 TCG list, Habakiri is Limited, Droll is Semi-Limited, Hornet Drones is Limited, and King of the Feral Imps is Forbidden. Master Duel lines can differ, so the TCG graph avoids relying on Feral Imps as the bridge.

    Mitsurugi starter

    Habakiri reveal into Saji

    i
    + +
    • 1Reveal Habakiri in hand; Special Summon Saji from Deck; resolve Habakiri by Tributing Saji.
    • 2Saji triggers because it was Tributed. Add Prayers when you need bodies or Ritual when you already have materials.
    • 3Prayers can search the missing Mitsurugi monster and turn a hand/GY Mitsurugi into a field body, setting up Murakumo or follow-up.
    + Chain discipline

    Do not default to the longest line. If Shizuku plus Prayers already beats the hand you expect, stop before walking into Nibiru.

    Search density

    Pre-Prep into Mirror package

    i
    + +
    • 1Pre-Preparation gets Mitsurugi Mirror and one named Mitsurugi Ritual Monster.
    • 2Use Habakiri to access Saji, then choose the Saji search based on what Pre-Prep already covered.
    • 3Mirror is your recovery Ritual Spell from hand or GY; Ritual is the power card that can summon from Deck.
    i Droll checkpoint

    Pre-Prep often forces Droll. If it resolves and the opponent does not Droll you, your later Engage or Prayers searches are more likely to resolve.

    Zero into body conversion

    Tips And Recovery

    Sequencing Tips

    • 1Start with Striker spells when your hand needs Engage value. Start with Pre-Prep when you want to test for Droll before committing Engage.
    • 2After a Ritual monster occupies a Main Monster Zone, plan how it leaves: Link into Camellia/Zeke, tribute with Prayers/Purification, or convert to Dyna Mondo.
    • 3Do not make Zero just because it is available. Kagari or Shizuku is often the tighter line when you need one exact Spell.
    • 4Track three Spells in GY. Engage draw changes borderline hands into winning hands.
    • 5Against open engine decks, pass on fewer summons with real interaction rather than building a 5+ summon board into Nibiru.

    Recovery Options

    • ARaye recurs after Striker Links leave by battle or opponent effect. Preserve her unless Linkage damage is lethal.
    • BKagari gets the best Spell back, usually Engage, Linkage, Widow Anchor, Shark Cannon, or Hornet Drones.
    • CKusanagi recovers a Mitsurugi card from GY or banishment, so it fixes Mirror/Ritual attrition and Shifter games better than most engines.
    • DMirror Ritual Summons from hand or GY and can shuffle itself back when your controlled Mitsurugi Ritual is Tributed.
    • EDyna Mondo converts Ritual monsters into recursion and gives you a clean Extra Monster Zone body.

    Side Deck Options And Counters

    This table favors cards seen across recent topping Sky Striker Mitsurugi lists plus current-format pressure points. Always re-check local legality before registering.
    Matchup / problem Side options How to use them
    Combo search decks: Kewl Tune, Mitsurugi, Yummy, Maliss
    		Droll after the first search. Fuwalos/Purulia before they chain bodies. Nibiru only when their line cannot stop short on interaction.
    Branded, Dracotail, GY recursion, Mirror lines
    		Ash the strongest starter, Belle the GY recovery or Kagari/Raye-style recursion, and Shark Cannon key GY bodies after forcing priority.
    Established boards and high-ATK monsters
    		Break first, then engage. Droplet sends extra spells for count; H.A.M.P. is searchable Striker removal; Lava Golem conflicts with Normal Summon, so board before committing.
    Backrow and trap decks
    		Thrust finds the exact breaker after they interact. Jamming Waves is searchable and keeps Striker spell count live.
    Going first into blowouts
    		Protect Shizuku or Ritual boards. Purification is best when you can tribute a Level 5+ Reptile and still profit from the tribute trigger.

    What Stops You

    Counter map
    • 1Ash on Pre-Prep or Engage denies the highest-value search.
    • 2Droll after Pre-Prep, Engage, or Prayers cuts both engines in half.
    • 3Imperm/Veiler on Saji or Kagari can leave you with bodies but no search.
    • 4Nibiru punishes full hybrid lines after the fifth summon.
    • 5Dimension Shifter hurts Striker GY loops more than Mitsurugi tribute triggers, so lean on Ritual pressure if it resolves.

    How You Counter Back

    Response map
    • 1Bait with a lower-value search when you already have the missing half.
    • 2Use Droplet and Linkage as spell-count builders, not only as board breakers.
    • 3Keep Raye in GY before passing so an opponent's removal can turn into another Ace.
    • 4When ahead, search Widow/Shark instead of chasing more combo pieces.
    • 5When behind, prioritize Murakumo, H.A.M.P., Evenly, TY-PHON, or Exciton over pure resource loops.

    Latest Tournament Variant Notes

    Exact hybrid

    Compact Mitsurugi Striker

    Recent exact hybrid tops often trim Mitsurugi to one Habakiri, one Saji, one Murakumo, three Prayers, one Ritual, and one Mirror. This lowers bricks and leaves room for Striker, Radiant Typhoon, and board breakers.

    Heavier engine

    Aramasa/Kusanagi/Futsu Build

    Heavier lists add Aramasa, Kusanagi, and Futsu to enable fuller Ritual loops, better recovery, and Great Purification boards. The tradeoff is more non-Striker pieces and more Main Monster Zone pressure.

    Current Striker trend

    Radiant Typhoon Sky Striker

    The latest high-profile Striker result found during research was Radiant Typhoon Sky Striker Top 4 at YCS Columbus 2026 on May 23. That supports keeping the Radiant Typhoon spell package in mind even when testing Mitsurugi.

    High ceiling

    Azamina 60-card Hybrid

    A Montevideo WCQ Regional-winning list used a 60-card Azamina/Mitsurugi/Sky Striker shell. It is powerful but much less suitable as a compact cheatsheet baseline because it adds a separate Sinful Spoils/Azamina decision tree.
