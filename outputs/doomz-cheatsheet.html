DoomZ

A top-to-bottom combo cheatsheet for the current DoomZ shell: destroy your own DoomZ and Power Patron cards, convert those triggers into Xyz bodies and Equip Spells, then end on Drastrius plus layered Power Patron interaction.

Last checked: 2026-06-08 Role: combo — 6 public MDM routes Win: Drastea into Drastrius, then Power Patron ceiling Latest top: Germany National Top 8, May 30, 2026

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

DoomZ is strongest when its own cards are destroyed by your effects. Every route is a resource loop: destroy, trigger, equip, Xyz, and turn face-up Extra Deck Power Patrons into searches. Going first, make Drastea equipped with D.O.O.M.D.U.R.G., then Xyz into Drastrius on the opponent's turn; ceiling lists add Varudras, Sargas, Regulus, and DoomZ Destruction.

  • 1Starters: Elara sets a DoomZ Spell/Trap, Amalthe searches a DoomZ monster, Change destroys a DoomZ from hand, Deck, or field to start the same chain.
  • 2Destroy payoff: Raiders destroys another DoomZ to add or Special Summon a DoomZ monster; if Raiders itself is destroyed by effect, it destroys a face-up monster.
  • 3Bridge: Drastea destroys a DoomZ card to summon itself and equip an Equip Spell — equipped Drastea is the opponent-turn Drastrius line.
  • 4Search lock: DoomZ Destruction attached to a DoomZ Xyz stops the opponent from adding cards from the Main Deck except by drawing.

Going Second And Core Constraint

Going second, Jupredo, A.D.R.A.S.T.E.I.A., Destruction, and Jupiter let the engine threaten pops, equip opposing monsters, and push direct damage without needing many generic board breakers.

Core constraint: after Raiders resolves, Extra Deck summons are Xyz-only for the rest of the turn. Top lists are built around this, using Rank 4/5/8/10 Xyz lines (Diactorus, Graflareio, Sargas, Merrymaker) instead of Link access. The Power Patron loop is the other half of the grammar: when Jupredo reaches the face-up Extra Deck, Terminus and Vidolium convert that state into more searches and bodies.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Detach or send A.D.R.A.S.T.E.I.A. early when the route wants it in GY; the public BLZD lines specifically value this around Graflareio.
  • 2Do not spend Raiders casually — you can use only one Raiders effect that turn, so decide whether the search/summon or the destroyed trigger matters more.
  • 3Keep a valid DoomZ card available for Drastea. Drastea without a destroy target is just a card in hand.
  • 4Against Fuwalos or Droll pressure, accept the compact Drastea/Drastrius board and keep follow-up instead of forcing the whole Power Patron chain.
  • 5DoomZ Destruction is strongest against decks that need add-to-hand chains; do not expose it to removal unless its destroyed trigger still advances your board.

Recovery Options

  • 1Power Patron DoomZ searches a DoomZ card when destroyed, turning removal on your own card into starter access.
  • 2Graflareio being destroyed summons a non-Xyz DoomZ from Deck or GY, so Raiders destroying it is often extension, not cost.
  • 3Change restocks the chain by destroying a DoomZ card from hand, Deck, or field, then can recover Destruction in longer lines.
  • 4Jupredo in the face-up Extra Deck is still a resource because Terminus and Vidolium convert it into more cards.
  • 5If the board is cleared, normal-summon Elara or Amalthe to rebuild through Raiders or Drastea rather than chasing a non-engine answer.

+ Side Deck Options And Counters

Matchup / problemSide optionsHow to use them
Combo mirrors: Kewl Tune, Yummy, Elfnote, DoomZ
Droll after the first add if it strands extension. Crow/Bystials hit GY extenders and DoomZ recovery. Shifter is strongest when your hand can still reach compact Drastea.
Branded / Dracotail / graveyard fusion decks
Trade on the GY card that converts into Fusion material or follow-up. If going second, pair board breakers with the engine's own equip-removal line.
Backrow and field-spell control
Use sweepers to clear before committing equips. Mid-Breaker protects combo turns; Anti-Spell and Solemn cards protect established boards from breakers.
Protected monsters and negate stacks
Remove the monster that stops Raiders, Drastea, or Graflareio from resolving. After the board is blanked, engine cards generate the follow-up body count.
Countering DoomZ directly
Hit the Graflareio/Raiders/Drastea conversion points (Ghost Ogre also works here). Do not destroy DoomZ Destruction unless you can handle its equip trigger. Removing Equip Spells before Drastea converts lowers the board sharply.

What Stops You

  • 1Early Droll: compact boards are still possible, but ceiling routes get much weaker.
  • 2Super Polymerization (into Garura or Mudragon) is a known problem for stacked monster boards.
  • 3Kaiju/Santa Claws answers an established Drastrius, Varudras, or protected monster before the opponent commits.
  • 4Backrow removal and banish effects that break the Equip Spell loop before Drastea converts.

How You Counter Back

  • 1Side the Solemn layer: Solemn Accusation and Solemn Judgment protect the board and trade into high-impact breakers.
  • 2Set Rotation + Magical Mid-Breaker Field protect combo turns and win field-spell fights.
  • 3Against hand traps, stop at the compact Drastea/Drastrius board and keep destroyed-trigger value (Power Patron DoomZ, Graflareio) as recovery.
  • 4Recent lists respect Super Poly through side planning and compact lines rather than overextending.
