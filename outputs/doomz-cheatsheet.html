DoomZ Mind Map

A top-to-bottom combo cheatsheet for the current DoomZ shell: destroy your own DoomZ and Power Patron cards, convert those triggers into Xyz bodies and Equip Spells, then end on Drastrius plus layered Power Patron interaction.

Playstyle And Win Condition

Combo Xyz locked often Engine removal destroys own cards Ceiling Drastrius + Varudras + Regulus

DoomZ is strongest when its cards are destroyed by your effects. Every route is a resource loop: destroy, trigger, equip, Xyz, and turn face-up Extra Deck Power Patrons into searches.

+ Win Going First

Make Drastea with D.O.O.M.D.U.R.G., then Xyz into Drastrius on the opponent's turn. The ceiling lists add Varudras, Sargas, Regulus, and DoomZ Destruction.

i Win Going Second

Jupredo, A.D.R.A.S.T.E.I.A., Destruction, and Jupiter let the engine threaten pops, equip opposing monsters, and push direct damage without needing many generic board breakers.

! Core Constraint

After Raiders resolves, Extra Deck summons are Xyz-only for the rest of the turn. Top lists are built around this, using Rank 4/5/8/10 Xyz lines instead of Link access.

Core Combos And Interactions

+ cards trigger together i destroy effects are value ! sequence around Droll and board breakers

Read this as the deck's trigger grammar. The graph section below shows the routes; this section explains why the cards chain together.

+ Starter Names

Elara sets a DoomZ Spell/Trap. Amalthe searches a DoomZ monster. Change can load a destroyed DoomZ from hand, Deck, or field to start the same chain.

+ Destroy Payoffs

Raiders destroys another DoomZ to add or Special Summon a DoomZ monster. If Raiders is destroyed by effect, it destroys a face-up monster.

i Drastea Bridge

Drastea destroys a DoomZ card to summon itself and equip an Equip Spell, usually D.O.O.M.D.U.R.G.. Equipped Drastea becomes the opponent-turn Drastrius line.

i Graflareio Pivot

Diactorus turns into Graflareio. Graflareio adds a DoomZ Spell/Trap and can destroy a card; when destroyed, it summons a non-Xyz DoomZ from Deck or GY.

+ Power Patron Loop

Jupredo is both extender and board breaker. When it reaches the face-up Extra Deck, Terminus and Vidolium convert that state into more searches and bodies.

! Search Lock

DoomZ Destruction attached to a DoomZ Xyz functions like a searchable anti-search effect: the opponent cannot add from Main Deck except by drawing.

Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Choke points Rebuild lines

The best DoomZ play is less about memorizing every ceiling route and more about knowing which trigger must survive this turn.

i Technical Tips

  • Detach or send A.D.R.A.S.T.E.I.A. early when the route wants it in GY; the public BLZD lines specifically value this around Graflareio.
  • Do not spend Raiders casually. You can use only one Raiders effect that turn, so decide whether the search/summon or the destroyed trigger matters more.
  • Keep a valid DoomZ card available for Drastea. Drastea without a destroy target is just a card in hand.
  • Against Fuwalos or Droll pressure, accept the compact Drastea/Drastrius board and keep follow-up instead of forcing the whole Power Patron chain.
  • DoomZ Destruction is strongest against decks that need add-to-hand chains; do not expose it to removal unless its destroyed trigger still advances your board.

+ Recovery Options

  • Power Patron DoomZ searches a DoomZ card when destroyed, turning removal on your own card into starter access.
  • Graflareio being destroyed summons a non-Xyz DoomZ from Deck or GY, so Raiders destroying it is often extension, not cost.
  • Change restocks the chain by destroying a DoomZ card from hand, Deck, or field, then can recover Destruction in longer lines.
  • Jupredo in the face-up Extra Deck is still a resource because Terminus and Vidolium convert it into more cards.
  • If the board is cleared, normal-summon Elara or Amalthe to rebuild through Raiders or Drastea rather than chasing a non-engine answer.
