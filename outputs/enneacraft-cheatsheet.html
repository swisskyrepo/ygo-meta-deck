Machine Flip Pendulum control — checked 2026-06-08

Enneacraft

A Machine Flip Pendulum control deck: build hidden information, convert Level 1 scales into searches, set reactive Level 9 traps, and close with Enneapolis burn or 3000 ATK pressure.

Last checked: 2026-06-08 UTC Role: reactive control Enneapolis burn plus Level 9 pressure TCG Advanced: May 18, 2026 list

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Enneacraft is not a fixed solitaire combo deck. It builds a face-down board that asks the opponent to choose between playing into hidden triggers or waiting while Enneapolis converts every flip into End Phase burn. Most lines aim for 2 Level 1s or 1 Level 1 plus Enneacraft Release, then convert scale searches into Atil.SPIA, Atori.MAR, and a matchup body.

  • 1Use Level 1 Enneacrafts as scales to pay 900 LP and reveal 3 Enneacraft cards. The random add still gives real card flow because most names are live.
  • 2Use Enneapolis to clear Pendulum Zones, recycle flipped monsters, and cash E.C. Counters for 900 damage each in the End Phase.
  • 3Win by layering Atori.MAR, Atil.SPIA, Asta.PIXEA, Aiza.LEON, or Archa.TAIL so the opponent cannot remove the board cleanly without taking searches, negates, bounce, hand pressure, or burn.

What The Opponent Must Respect

  • 1Targeting your cards can flip Asta.PIXEA, negate the effect, and banish a random card face-down from their hand.
  • 2Destroying cards can flip Archa.TAIL, protecting your monsters and Enneacraft Spells for the turn.
  • 3Responding to your activation can flip Aiza.LEON and bounce up to 3 cards. On your turn, field effects can run into Atori.MAR's negate.
  • 4The small trigger matrix: Ekto flips on effects that include Special Summoning, Deftero on Deck-to-hand effects, Enato on effects in GY or banishment, and Trito on effects that Set cards.

Wrong Level 1 name

When the opened small name does not match the opposing deck's likely first action, put it in scale instead, use Release to place a better name, and accept a lower ceiling rather than adding dead unknowns.

Reverth grind branch

With 1 starter plus Reverth, convert extra monsters into draws and leave the GY trigger loaded for the opponent's summon. Smaller board, but it plays through a disrupted opener.

Subspace Battle pivot

Seen as a 3-of in the June 6 Lubbock winner: use 3000 ATK Level 9s to win reveal checks and add the exact big body you need when a scale line missed it.

Fallen & Virtuous board break

Compact non-engine removal backed by Extra Deck sends — the answer when the opponent presents a face-up card you cannot beat by waiting, or refuses to trigger your face-downs.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Search for what the board is missing, not for a fixed script. The normal target order is Field or Reset, then Reverth, then the exact Level 9 trap.
  • 2Do not put both scales into names you cannot pick up. Enneapolis returning Pendulum cards is the main way to keep the chain of searches moving.
  • 3Against Droll, take the last search Proto gives you, use Release to place the most relevant scale, and stop trying to snowball.
  • 4Keep LP math visible. Multiple 900 LP scale searches are normal, but they make enemy OTK lines and your own Enneapolis burn clock more important.
  • 5Subspace Battle is strongest when it finds a Level 9 you will set, not when it locks the exact name you need to activate immediately.

Recovery Options

  • 1Reverth refreshes hands and then threatens to flip up or set another Enneacraft after the opponent summons.
  • 2Reset turns face-up Enneacrafts face-down from GY so the same monsters can threaten another flip window.
  • 3Release, from GY after the turn it was sent, adds a face-up Enneacraft monster from Extra Deck back to hand.
  • 4Enneapolis returns face-up Pendulum Monster Cards, protecting value and reopening scales.
  • 5The F&V/Fossil package gives you removal when the opponent refuses to trigger your face-downs.

Build lesson

More names, less mystery loss

Post-Blazing Dominion lists gain Trito and Atil, improving the number of live names and giving the deck a stronger chain-link punishment plan. The tradeoff is more exact sequencing and more cards that punish careless name locks.

Ghost Ogre on Enneapolis is a known counter. If you expect it, hold Archa for destruction protection, Aiza for bounce lines, or Asta when they target.
Established boards and monsters over 3000 ATK
High-ATK monsters can walk over set Level 9s. Use non-targeting negation, Sphere Mode, F&V, Reset's face-up negate, or Book effects to avoid losing the set board in combat.
Maliss, Sky Striker, DoomZ, non-target removal
TCGplayer's counter article flags Maliss non-target banish, Sky Striker Lemnisgate bounce, and DoomZ Destruction as real problems. Side to stop the engine, not just to protect a single face-down.
Backrow and slow control
Trito gives in-engine S/T pressure. Cosmic and Duster answer floodgates. Daruma resets awkward boards without targeting and keeps the game in face-down territory.

What Stops You

  • 1Droll after the first search ends the usual scale-chain turn. It still lets Proto trigger once if Droll was activated from hand.
  • 2Ash on Reverth is stronger than Ash on a random Level 1 when you already have multiple searchers.
  • 3Forbidden Droplet and Forbidden Crown are non-targeting negates that Enneacraft monsters cannot usually answer with their own monster effects.
  • 4Passive play reduces your Enneapolis counters. Opponents can wait, attack over 2500 DEF bodies, then force you to flip on their terms.

How You Counter Back

  • 1When ahead, stop searching and pass with stronger unknowns. A mystery Atori or Asta is often worth more than one more random add.
  • 2Against patient players, Flip Summon Asta or Archa yourself to make 3000 ATK pressure and force their removal.
  • 3Use Reset from GY before your mandatory FLIP effect resolves if you need to dodge monster negation by flipping back down.
  • 4Side into hard board breakers going second, because Enneacraft is strongest when the opponent is the one forced to act first.
