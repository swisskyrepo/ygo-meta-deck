i Playstyle and win condition

Banish is your extension zone

Maliss turns banishing into tempo. The main-deck monsters pay 300 LP to return from banishment, while Link monsters convert those bodies into protected interruptions, removal, and OTK pressure.

Game plan

  • Open a starter, banish a Maliss name, then use the self-summon trigger to build Link material without spending many cards.
  • Use Red Ransom and Maliss in Underground to reach the missing engine piece, then climb through Cyberse links.
  • Convert excess bodies into Hearts Crypter, Firewall/S:P style interaction, Heatsoul draw pressure, or Accesscode/Allied Code lethal.
  • Keep a banished or graveyard Maliss name available so Bystials, traps, and Underground become follow-up instead of one-shot combo tools.

Win condition

1 Starter

Dormouse, Rabbit, Underground, Gold Sarc, or Backup @Ignister finds the first banish.

+ Loop

Banished Maliss monsters revive themselves and become Link material.

i Control

Traps, Hearts Crypter, Firewall, S:P, and hand traps compress the opponent's turn.

! Kill

Accesscode, Allied Code, or a powered Hearts Crypter closes once the board is simplified.
