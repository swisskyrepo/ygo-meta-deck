Banish-to-extend combo — checked June 2026

Maliss

A top-to-bottom reference for the banish-to-extend engine and the current @Ignister/Bystial tournament builds: combo pivots, end boards, recovery lines, and matchup side options.

Checked June 2026 Role: combo-control Win: link climb into Accesscode lethal TCG: Apollousa forbidden (May 18, 2026 list)

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Maliss turns banishing into tempo. The main-deck monsters pay 300 LP to return from banishment, while the Link monsters convert those bodies into protected interruptions, removal, and OTK pressure.

  • 1Open a starter, banish a Maliss name, then use the self-summon trigger to build Link material without spending many cards.
  • 2Use Red Ransom and Maliss in Underground to reach the missing engine piece, then climb through Cyberse Links.
  • 3Convert excess bodies into Hearts Crypter, Firewall Dragon/S:P Little Knight style interaction, Heatsoul draw pressure, or Accesscode Talker/Allied Code Talker @Ignister lethal.
  • 4Keep a banished or graveyard Maliss name available so Bystials, traps, and Underground become follow-up instead of one-shot combo tools.

Win Condition / Format Notes

  • 1Starter: Dormouse, White Rabbit, Underground, Gold Sarcophagus, or Backup @Ignister finds the first banish.
  • 2Loop: banished Maliss monsters revive themselves and become Link material.
  • 3Control: traps, Hearts Crypter, Firewall Dragon, S:P Little Knight, and hand traps compress the opponent's turn.
  • 4Kill: Accesscode Talker, Allied Code Talker @Ignister, or a powered Hearts Crypter closes once the board is simplified.
  • 5Format: current TCG builds run @Ignister + Bystial + Dominus. The Master Duel spreadsheet route ends on Apollousa, which is not legal in TCG Advanced as of the May 18, 2026 list.

Backup @Ignister pivot

When the normal summon is a hand trap or any spare body. Backup is a bridge, not the whole starter: it supplies the missing Cyberse tuner and searches Wizard, turning the normal summon into Link material toward Red Ransom.

Gold Sarcophagus opener

Sarc is a starter because banishing a Maliss is a delayed search and an immediate extender. Banish the name whose self-summon fixes the current hand — not always the flashiest one. Strong under Droll since it summons before stacking searches.

Bystial extender

After a DARK Maliss hits the GY as Link material, a Bystial banishes it, summons itself, and re-enables the 300 LP self-summon — extension plus disruption in one card.

Master Duel trap loop

MD-only: White Binder plus GWC-06 converts a banished Link into revived pressure and a set interruption, ending on Apollousa. Not legal in current TCG Advanced.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Placement matters: Hearts Crypter is best when it points to a monster. Put Ransom/Crypter/other Links in zones that keep arrows live before committing to interruption.
  • 2Current TCG is not the MD combo: Apollousa remains forbidden in TCG Advanced as of the May 18, 2026 list. Use Heatsoul, Firewall, S:P, and Allied Code routes in paper.
  • 3Rabbit target is contextual: TB-11 gives a body from Deck; MTP-07 searches or banishes. Pick the trap that fixes the hand, not the one you memorized first.
  • 4Bystials are extenders: after linking away a DARK Maliss, Magnamhut both summons itself and moves that Maliss to banish for the 300 LP self-summon.
  • 5Play under Droll: when Droll & Lock Bird is likely, value Gold Sarc, Underground, and already-opened extenders. Do not stack unnecessary searches before you have a board.
  • 6Hold the real follow-up: Hare in hand or a live Chessy Cat draw keeps the next turn playable after a board breaker. Do not spend every Maliss name for a slightly larger board.

Recovery Options

  • 1Normal summon negated: Dormouse is not an on-summon effect, so force the opponent to answer the activation. If stopped, pivot through Underground, Backup, or Sarc.
  • 2Rabbit stopped: create banish pressure another way — Gold Sarc banishes a name, a Bystial banishes a DARK Maliss from GY, or Underground restarts.
  • 3Nibiru reset: keep one set trap or banished Maliss before the fifth summon when possible. The Nibiru token can still become Link Spider, but only if you preserved follow-up.
  • 4Droll turn: stop chasing extra searches. Convert existing bodies into Heatsoul or Hearts, then lean on Dominus, Impermanence, and remaining hand traps.
  • 5Artifact Lancea: Lancea is one of the hardest counters because it shuts off banish costs and self-summons. Make a minimal Cyberse board and pass with interaction.
  • 6Board wiped: Underground, TB-11, and Bystials rebuild from spent names. Prioritize keeping a Maliss in GY or banished over emptying all zones for damage.

+ Side Deck Options And Counters

Use this both ways: what Maliss sides into the field, and what opponents should side against Maliss.
Matchup / problemSide optionsHow to use them
Against combo
Use Droll and Nibiru to cap explosive turns. Fuwalos/Purulia punish extension. Dominus Impulse and Impermanence answer key monster effects.
Against GY and DARK decks
Bystials double as extenders and disruption. Ghost Belle checks GY effects. Dimensional Fissure and Different Dimension Ground punish GY decks while Maliss can still use banished names.
Against backrow / control
Duster and Lightning Storm clear backrow. Talent punishes interaction. Anti-Spell is a going-first lock piece; Accesscode converts a cleared lane into lethal.
What opponents side vs Maliss
Lancea is the cleanest turn stop. Nibiru punishes the long climb. Ogre on Underground, Ash on Backup/Underground, Imperm on Chessy/Hare, and Droll after the first search all force lower boards.

What Stops You

  • 1Artifact Lancea shuts off banish costs and self-summons — the hardest single counter.
  • 2Nibiru punishes the long Link climb if you commit five summons without protection.
  • 3Droll & Lock Bird after the first search caps Ransom/Backup search chains.
  • 4Ghost Ogre and Ash Blossom on Underground/Backup; Imperm on Chessy/Hare.

How You Counter Back

  • 1Against Lancea, make a minimal Cyberse board and pass with hand-trap interaction instead of forcing the climb.
  • 2Against Nibiru, bank a set trap or a banished Maliss before the fifth summon; the token can become Link Spider.
  • 3Against Droll, lead with Gold Sarc/Underground summon pressure before stacking searches; settle for Heatsoul plus hand traps.
  • 4Hold Hare in hand or a live Chessy draw so board breakers do not end the game; rebuild with TB-11 and Bystials.
