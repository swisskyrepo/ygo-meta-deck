i Playstyle and win condition

Turn one pressure, turn three inevitability

Primite Blue-Eyes wins by converting a small starter into Dragon interruptions, then using True Light, Roar, Beryl, and Lode to keep replaying Blue-Eyes bodies while Drillbeam trades with the opponent's best face-up card.

Primary win line

Use Wishes to start the Blue-Eyes engine, place True Light, make Spirit Dragon lines, and end on Ultimate Spirit plus Sifr or a lower-risk Spirit plus Seal board.

Primite role

Beryl and Lode are not just extenders. They give Blue-Eyes a normal-monster body, recurring Beryl, and Drillbeam access when the full combo is stopped.

Latest top variant

The latest full TCG list found is Addy Delplanque's 43-card Bystial Primite Blue-Eyes Top 4 at Manchester WCQ Regional on April 18, 2026. A newer May 2 OCG Malaysia result records Primite Blue-Eyes in Top 8 but without a public list.

Best starter Wishes for Eyes of Blue plus discard turns into Sage, Roar, True Light, and the first Blue-Eyes body.
Best backup Normal Summon Beryl sets Lode, gets Drillbeam, and stores Beryl in GY for the next Standby Phase.
Format posture Rogue in current TCG data. Side for Kewl Tune, Branded, DoomZ, Dracotail, Maliss, Sky Striker, and Yummy.
Risk point Lode's Normal Monster summon locks field effects of Special Summoned monsters for the turn. Sequence it after critical field effects.
