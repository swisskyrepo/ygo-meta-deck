Primite Blue-Eyes

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Turn one pressure, turn three inevitability. Primite Blue-Eyes wins by converting a small starter into Dragon interruptions, then using True Light, Roar of the Blue-Eyed Dragons, Beryl, and Lode to keep replaying Blue-Eyes White Dragon bodies while Primite Drillbeam trades with the opponent's best face-up card.

  • 1Primary line: Wishes for Eyes of Blue starts the engine, places True Light, makes Blue-Eyes Spirit Dragon lines, and ends on Ultimate Spirit plus Sifr or a lower-risk Spirit plus Seal board.
  • 2Primite role: Beryl and Lode are not just extenders — they give Blue-Eyes a Normal Monster body, recurring Beryl, and Drillbeam access when the full combo is stopped.
  • 3Going second, Wishes turns a Blue-Eyes body into Blue-Eyes Tyrant Dragon, a trap-immune attacker that closes simplified boards.

Win Condition And Format Notes

  • 1Best starter: Wishes for Eyes of Blue plus a discard turns into Sage, Roar, True Light, and the first Blue-Eyes body.
  • 2Best backup: Normal Summon Beryl sets Lode, gets Drillbeam, and stores Beryl in GY for the next Standby Phase.
  • 3Format posture: rogue in current TCG data. Side for Kewl Tune, Branded, DoomZ, Dracotail, Maliss, Sky Striker, and Yummy.
  • 4Risk point: Lode's Normal Monster summon locks field effects of your Special Summoned monsters for the turn. Sequence it after critical field effects.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Lode timing is the deck's biggest skill check. Lode's Normal Monster summon stops your Special Summoned monsters from activating field effects for the turn — resolve Spirit with Eyes of Blue, Crimson Dragon, and other key effects first.
  • 2Wishes has two jobs. Wishes gives the tuner and the Blue-Eyes summon spell, then later becomes the equip that unlocks Tyrant Dragon.
  • 3Do not auto-push Crimson Dragon. Stop at Spirit plus Seal or Spirit plus set interaction when the opponent represents Nibiru, Droll, or board wipes.
  • 4Drillbeam needs a live reveal condition. With a Normal Monster on field or a Level 5+ Primite monster, Drillbeam needs no reveal; otherwise keep a Primite card or Normal Monster in hand.
  • 5True Light is power and liability. True Light protects Blue-Eyes from targeting and supplies follow-up, but if it leaves the field it destroys your monsters — respect Harpie's Feather Duster and Ghost Ogre-style answers.
  • 6Top lists lean on hand traps. Recent builds use the Blue-Eyes engine as a compact threat package plus many slots of Ash Blossom, Effect Veiler, Droll, Nibiru, and Dominus Purge — midrange boards beat all-in combo hands.

Recovery Options

  • 1Beryl Standby loop: with Beryl in GY and a Normal Monster on field or in GY, add Beryl back in your Standby Phase; Normal it again to set another Primite Spell/Trap.
  • 2True Light reload: True Light summons Blue-Eyes or sets a Blue-Eyes spell/trap from Deck; Roar reaches Deck, GY, and banishment.
  • 3Ultimate Spirit floats: destroyed Ultimate Spirit brings back a LIGHT Dragon from GY; Jet Dragon protects other cards and recurs when cards are destroyed.
  • 4Bystial and Seal bridge: Magnamhut searches a Dragon in the End Phase; Seal converts any Dragon pair into a bounce plus a Deck summon (Neo Kaiser for combo access).
  • 5Primite S/T recursion: Drillbeam can set itself from GY while you control a Primite monster; Primite Roar keeps Normal Monsters alive through battle and destruction.
  • 6Fusion cleanup: Blue-Eyes materials in GY turn Ultimate Fusion into a removal spell that also creates a threat like Twin Burst.

Branded / Dracotail
Contest GY and Fusion access. Belle and Called by the Grave stop key graveyard effects; Different Dimension Ground or Shifter force awkward lines. Super Poly turns their board into your removal via Mudragon or Garura.

Shadow-Imprisoning Mirror is narrow but high-impact against DARK effect chains. Bystials and Belle apply pressure while doubling as Dragon material.
Backrow and control
Use removal before committing True Light. Tyrant is strong into trap decks once floodgates are gone, and Evenly Matched can reset boards Blue-Eyes cannot attack through.
Mirror and Dragon boards
Droll after Wishes, Shifter before GY setup, and backrow removal on True Light are the cleanest mirror punishes. Branded variants can use Fallen of Albaz into Alba-Lenatus lines.
Going first protection
Crossout Designator and Forbidden Lance protect the first starter; Talents and Thrust punish monster effects; Solemn Strike is a clean option into Normal Summon or Extra Deck choke points.

What Stops You

  • 1Droll & Lock Bird stops the chain of Sage, Mausoleum, and follow-up searches after the first add resolves.
  • 2Nibiru punishes the double Spirit Dragon and Crimson line.
  • 3GY denial — Dimension Shifter, Called by the Grave, Ghost Belle, Different Dimension Ground — cuts off Maiden, Roar, Beryl, Ultimate Spirit, and True Light follow-up.
  • 4Backrow removal — Feather Duster, Lightning Storm, Ghost Ogre — because True Light leaving the field can wipe your monsters.

How You Counter Back

  • 1Route lower into shown hand traps: end on Spirit Dragon plus Seal plus a set Drillbeam instead of the Crimson push.
  • 2Crossout Designator and Forbidden Lance protect the first search; Called by the Grave pre-empts GY hand traps.
  • 3Pivot to Primite control when Blue-Eyes is stopped: Beryl recursion and a live Drillbeam only need a Normal Monster on field or in GY.
  • 4Protect or sequence around True Light so you are never all-in on it against backrow removal.
