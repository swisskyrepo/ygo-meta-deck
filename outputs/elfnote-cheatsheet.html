i Playstyle and win condition

Take the center, convert one starter into layered interruptions

Primary Plan

Special Summon a main Elfnote to the center MMZ, use Power Patron to Synchro climb, then loop Welcome Home, Regina, and Fortuna into Rhapsodia.

Patron Welcome Rhapsodia

Win Condition

End on a mix of omni-negates, monster/S/T checks, Rhapsodia face-up negate, center-zone bounce/hand rip, and opponent-turn Synchro pressure. The deck wins by denying the first push and converting surviving Elfnote bodies into lethal Synchro damage.

Main Risks

Fuwalos/Maxx "C" force reduced lines. Blocking or occupying the center MMZ can disable key self-summons. Going second, the engine needs non-engine help because Power Patron is fragile into established removal.

i Tips

Latest Meta Notes

Sequence around Fuwalos

MDM combo records include dedicated Lucina vs Fuwa and Medius vs Fuwa routes. Trade ceiling for fewer draws: Lucina can stop at Baronne + Rhapsodia after one draw; Medius can stop at Ecclesia + Rhapsodia after two.

Respect Droll

Lucina and Power Patron search early. If the hand can start with Tinia or Welcome Home, you can place instead of repeatedly adding. GalacticJoey's Medius notes mention adding Tinia instead of Lucina as a lower-ceiling Droll concession.

Center MMZ matters

The MDM guide flags zone awkwardness as a real weakness. Do not leave your own center MMZ occupied by a non-converting body before resolving the Elfnote self-summons and opponent-turn movement effects.

Regina is the opponent-turn fuse

Keep Welcome Home face-up or an expendable Elfnote ready so Regina can send it, Special Summon herself, then access another Elfnote to turn Rhapsodia and Seraphim live.

Fiendsmith raises ceiling

Fiendsmith lines convert Armades/Moon access into Requiem, Engraver, Sequence, Lacrima, and Caesar before returning to the Elfnote trap line. This protects later plays and gives a high-impact end board.

Latest variants

Master Duel's June 5 sample was a 60-card Adventure/Grass/Fiendsmith Elfnote list. The latest OCG tournament top found was a 40-card Elfnote build in Asia WCQ Hong Kong, showing the deck can also stay lean and Synchro-focused.

+ Recovery options

When the first push breaks

Rhapsodia Rebuild

Use Rhapsodia to send a monster from hand or field, revive an Elfnote with a different Attribute, and turn any surviving Synchro into a face-up negate. This also reloads the center MMZ for Lucina/Tinia/Fortuna quick effects.

Regina Hand Return

Regina adds herself back when used as Synchro material. Hold her as a flexible opponent-turn extender: send Welcome Home or another Elfnote, summon to center, then summon Lucina, Tinia, or Fortuna from Deck.

Seraphim Converts Scraps

Seraphim revives a Level 6 or lower Elfnote and can alter levels across the field. Use it to turn leftover Patron/Fortuna/Regina bodies into Dragster, Crystal Wing, Herald, Omega, or another Synchro layer.

Medius From GY

Medius can shuffle a hand or face-up monster into Deck to Special Summon itself from the GY. That gives post-interruption material and helps recycle awkward garnets such as Jet Synchron lines.

Omega and B2B Grind

Omega can shuffle itself and another GY card back, while Kewl Tune B2B can convert a Tuner in GY/banishment into immediate Synchro pressure on the opponent's turn.

Walbaz Follow-up

Walbaz lines set up Ecclesia and Fallen of the White Dragon access. The MDM combo records use this branch for Albaz pressure on the opponent's turn and End Phase follow-up.
