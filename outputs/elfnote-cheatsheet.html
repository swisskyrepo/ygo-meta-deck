Resilient Synchro combo — checked June 8, 2026

Elfnote

A Synchro deck built around center Main Monster Zone movement: Elfnote Power Patron level changes, Elfnotes: Welcome Home deck summons, Regina/Fortuna access, and Rhapsodia of Madness follow-up.

Last checked: 2026-06-08 Role: resilient Synchro combo Win: layered negates into Synchro lethal Latest OCG top: 2nd, Asia WCQ Hong Kong

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Take the center and convert one starter into layered interruptions. Special Summon a main Elfnote to the center Main Monster Zone, use Power Patron to Synchro climb, then loop Welcome Home, Regina, and Fortuna into Rhapsodia.

  • 1Lucina searches Power Patron; Patron raises levels, Synchro climbs, and searches another Elfnote as material.
  • 2Tinia places Welcome Home, which sends a body to bring Regina to center and start deck summons.
  • 3Fortuna places Rhapsodia: a send converts into an Elfnote reborn plus a face-up negate while a Synchro is live.
  • 4Seraphim Strelitzia revives a Level 6 or lower Elfnote and modulates levels for extra Synchro layers.

Win Condition And Main Risks

End on a mix of omni-negates, monster and Spell/Trap checks, a Rhapsodia face-up negate, center-zone bounce and hand rip, and opponent-turn Synchro pressure. The deck wins by denying the first push and converting surviving Elfnote bodies into lethal Synchro damage.

Mulcharmy Fuwalos and Maxx "C" force reduced lines. Blocking or occupying the center Main Monster Zone can disable the key self-summons. Going second, the engine needs non-engine help because Power Patron is fragile into established removal.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Sequence around Fuwalos. MDM has dedicated Lucina-vs-Fuwa and Medius-vs-Fuwa routes: Lucina can stop at Baronne + Rhapsodia after one draw; Medius at Ecclesia + Rhapsodia after two.
  • 2Respect Droll. Lucina and Patron search early. If the hand can start with Tinia or Welcome Home, place instead of repeatedly adding; searching Tinia instead of Lucina is a lower-ceiling Droll concession.
  • 3Center MMZ matters. Do not leave your own center zone occupied by a non-converting body before resolving the Elfnote self-summons and opponent-turn movement effects.
  • 4Regina is the opponent-turn fuse. Keep Welcome Home face-up or an expendable Elfnote ready so Regina can send it, summon herself, and turn Rhapsodia and Seraphim live.
  • 5Fiendsmith raises the ceiling. Armades/Moon access converts into Requiem, Engraver, Sequence, Lacrima, and Caesar before returning to the Elfnote trap line.

Recovery Options

  • 1Rhapsodia rebuild: send from hand or field, revive an Elfnote with a different Attribute, and turn any surviving Synchro into a face-up negate; this also reloads the center zone for Lucina/Tinia/Fortuna quick effects.
  • 2Regina hand return: she adds herself back when used as Synchro material — hold her as a flexible opponent-turn extender into deck summons.
  • 3Seraphim converts scraps: revive a Level 6 or lower Elfnote and alter levels to turn leftover bodies into Dawn Dragster, Crystal Wing, Herald, or Omega.
  • 4Medius from GY: shuffle a hand or face-up monster into Deck to Special Summon itself, recycling awkward garnets such as Jet Synchron.
  • 5Grind tools: Omega shuffles itself plus a GY card back; Kewl Tune B2B converts a Tuner in GY/banishment into opponent-turn Synchro pressure; the Walbaz branch keeps Ecclesia and Fallen of the White Dragon follow-up live.

What Stops You

  • 1Fuwalos and Maxx "C" force reduced lines and cap the ceiling.
  • 2Blocking or occupying the center Main Monster Zone disables the Elfnote self-summons.
  • 3Droll & Lock Bird punishes the early Lucina/Patron search chain.
  • 4Going second, Power Patron is fragile into established removal without non-engine help.

How You Counter Back

  • 1Take the dedicated vs-Fuwa routes: one or two draws into Baronne/Ecclesia plus Rhapsodia.
  • 2Caesar first checks Nibiru-style punishment before you extend.
  • 3Regina + Rhapsodia rebuild interruption on the opponent's turn even after a broken first push.
  • 4Fortuna bounces face-up Spell/Traps and Lucina bounces monsters in the right level window.
