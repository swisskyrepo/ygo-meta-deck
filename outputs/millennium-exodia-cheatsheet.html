Rogue control / OTK — checked June 2026

Millennium Exodia Incarnate

A compact cheatsheet for the current Millennium Exodia shell: pure Ankh lines, Golem/Wedju protection, Heart of Gaia pressure, and the Fiendsmith/Odion branches showing up in recent Master Duel lists. Summon Exodia, protect the turn, cash out with Blaze.

Updated: June 8, 2026 Role: rogue control / OTK Win: Ankh into Unstoppable, then Exxod or Blaze Variant: 50-60 card Fiendsmith / Gaia / Odion shells

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Millennium Exodia is not a five-pieces-in-hand FTK deck. It is a low-combo control deck that turns multiple starters into Millennium Ankh, summons The Unstoppable Exodia Incarnate, then uses End Phase access to Exxod Fires of Rage or Obliterate!!! Blaze. The boss cannot be destroyed by opponent card effects, negates one Spell/Trap activation each turn, and scales in battle from your LP.

  • 1Find Ankh through Shield of the Millennium Dynasty, Heart of the Blue-Eyes, Sengenjin chains, Wedju Temple, or raw draw.
  • 2Resolve Ankh after you have used any Extra Deck or non-Millennium lines you need that turn.
  • 3Set Exxod going first; take Blaze when a Battle Phase kill is realistic.

Win Paths And Current Position

  • 1Exodia attacks over boards with LP-based ATK; a resolved Ankh shuffles itself back into the Deck, so repeat summons are real.
  • 2Blaze equips all five pieces for +10000 ATK and piercing after backrow is cleared; Exxod wipes the opponent's field while you control a Level 10+ Exodia monster.
  • 3Rogue, not Tier 1: current top decks force multiple hand traps and backup engines. Recent Master Duel lists trend toward 50-60 cards with Fiendsmith, Gaia, and many hand traps because the mandatory Exodia pieces brick exactly-40 builds.
  • 4Latest sourced TCG placement: Concepcion WCQ Regional Top 8, February 7, 2026.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Use Extra Deck value before Ankh. Ankh locks Summons after resolution and shuffles face-up non-Millennium bodies by text.
  • 2Lead Wedju into K9 pressure. Recent notes call out K9 Izuna punishing hand/GY monster effects; Wedju Temple changes the first interaction point.
  • 3Golem protects activation, not everything. Golem stops Ankh activation negation, but effects that negate Ankh's effect can still matter.
  • 4Blaze is usually last. Blaze prevents your other card effects for the rest of the turn after its equip/wipe mode.
  • 5Track LP before battle. Paying 2000 for multiple Millennium summons lowers Exodia's battle ceiling; revealing Ankh saves LP.
  • 6Hide the Exodia plan when possible. Do not expose it early if your hand is Gaia/Fiendsmith. Set Exxod first going first, and expect non-destruction removal — banish, bounce, absorb, tribute, or send answers the boss better than destruction.

Recovery Options

  • 1Repeat Ankh. Ankh shuffles itself into the Deck instead of going to the GY; Shield and Heart of the Blue-Eyes can find more copies and reload Exodia.
  • 2Pieces back. Exxod adds a Forbidden One piece (Exodia the Forbidden One and the four limbs) from Deck/GY or shuffles up to five from GY/banishment; Obliterate!!! can add a piece back when it leaves the Spell/Trap Zone.
  • 3Rebuild bodies. Wedju replaces destruction of face-up Millennium monsters as Continuous Spells; the main monsters re-place themselves from hand and summon from the Spell/Trap Zone.
  • 4Fiendsmith grind. Engraver recurs by recycling another LIGHT Fiend; Lacrima, Desirae, and Agnumday keep pressure when Exodia is delayed or removed.
  • 5Heart check. Heart of the Blue-Eyes is once per Duel from GY after Ankh resolved: send a Level 8+ or 3000+ ATK summon to the GY and Special Summon itself.
  • 6Side-aware recovery. Pure Millennium tolerates Dimension Shifter; in Fiendsmith hands, Ghost Belle / Called by the Grave are cleaner anti-GY tools.

+ Side Deck Options And Counters

Adjust for format legality. In Master Duel this is a flexible-card map rather than a literal 15-card side deck.
Matchup / problemSide optionsHow to use them
Kewl Tune — current Tier 1 power deck
Stop chained searches, hand knowledge, and Synchro setup. Droll is the highest ceiling after the first add; Droplet / Book of Eclipse force a weaker board before Ankh. Nibiru quality depends on their line, and do not cut too many starters — you still need Ankh access.
K9 / Vanquish Soul K9 — Izuna punishes hand/GY monster effects
Recent Exodia notes call out K9 Izuna as a major issue. Lead Wedju if opened, then use Droplet or Triple Tactics Talent after they interact. Super Polymerization was cut in one recent list for being unreliable into VS-K9 boards.
Dracotail / Branded — fusion grind and bounce pressure
They can answer a lone Exodia with bounce or layered fusion pressure. Force a weaker board, then punish overextension with Exxod. Do not rely only on Exodia's destruction protection; non-destruction removal is the real threat.
Backrow / trap decks — Odion, Labrynth, floodgates
Cosmic Cyclone banishes high-value traps; Evenly Matched resets wide fields. Blaze can clear Spell/Trap Zones when Exodia is ready to kill, but it locks your effects afterward — sequence removal before activating it.
GY combo — Fiendsmith mirrors, Orcust, recursive engines
Dimension Shifter is strong in pure Millennium hands but can turn off your own Engraver / Tract lines — side it only when the payoff is worth it. Belle and Called by the Grave are cleaner when you need your own Fiendsmith GY.
Going second into boards — resolving Ankh after trades
Draw punishers and board breakers force interaction before the Ankh commit. Lava Golem-type cards answer towers or stacked negates, but consume your Normal Summon — which conflicts with Heart of Gaia and The Man with the Mark.

What Stops You

  • 1Ash Blossom, Imperm, or Effect Veiler on Shield / Sengenjin / Golem stops Ankh access before it starts.
  • 2Droll after the first search turns off the chain of Shield, Sengenjin, Wedju, and Fiendsmith searches.
  • 3Negating Ankh's effect still matters; Golem only says the activation cannot be negated.
  • 4Bounce, banish, absorb, tribute, or send Exodia — destruction is the weakest removal type against it.
  • 5Attacking the LP total: Millennium costs lower Exodia's battle ATK ceiling and speed up the standby drain clock.

How You Counter Back

  • 1Start with hand traps that trade into the current top decks, then add board breakers if going second feels unwinnable.
  • 2Keep the starter count high enough that you still see two playable engine cards after siding.
  • 3Use Triple Tactics Talent and Called by the Grave to force Ankh through hand-trap-heavy fields.
