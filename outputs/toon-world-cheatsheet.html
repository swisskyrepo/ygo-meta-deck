Toon World

After Revolution Booster, Toon is the most-played OCG deck — 44 of 173 tops (25.4%) in OCG 2026.04. Competitive builds loop Funny Dark Rabbit and Toon World the Perfect World into a multi-disruption combo board; classic Toon Kingdom lines still win through direct attacks, Comic Hand steals, and the Perfect World search chain.

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Assemble a live "Toon World" substitute, convert searches into bodies, protect the field spell, then end games by attacking directly over the opponent's board.

  • 1Turn on the book. Toon Kingdom and Toon World the Perfect World are both treated as Toon World in the Field Zone, which enables most Toon text. Toon Table of Contents finds Toon Bookmark or any Toon card; Bookmark finds anything that mentions Toon World, including Kingdom and Comic Hand. Terraforming is a fifth copy of the field spell.
  • 2Convert searchers into pressure. Toon Page-Flip, Red-Eyes Toon Dragon, Toon Dark Magician, and The Black Stone of Legend turn one enabled field spell into multiple Toon bodies or a Toon Spell/Trap search.
  • 3Protect, steal, and end. Toon Terror protects the field spell and monster effects, Toon Briefcase punishes summons, and Comic Hand plus Shadow Toon let you go second without clearing every monster first.

Win Condition And Format Notes

The win condition is direct damage: Toon monsters attack directly while the book is up, so opposing monster walls matter less than whether the opponent can banish, bounce, or negate your field spell.

  • 1Toon is the #1 OCG 2026.04 deck — 44 of 173 tops (25.4%), ahead of Kewl Tune (19.7%), across 29 events from 30 May–14 June 2026 (Road of the King report #9-11). The 44 split as 34 Toon, 7 Toon FTK, 2 Ryzeal Toon, and 1 Dark Magician Toon.
  • 2Two competitive builds: an Expansion build that loops Toon World the Perfect World ③ and Blue-Eyes Toon Ultimate Dragon recursion into ~9 disruptions, and a Midrange build that cuts brick Toons for hand traps like Maxx "C" and ends on ~4 disruptions. Top finishes: Xiao Wu Wu Yi 10-2 (1-4th) and Honey Pomelo Tea 9-2-1 (5-8th) at China Mainland WCQ 2026 OCG Shanghai.
  • 3The RV01 Revolution Booster support (Funny Dark Rabbit, Comic Cat, Evil Box, Mind Scan, Blue-Eyes Toon Ultimate Dragon) released in the OCG on May 30, 2026 and is not guaranteed TCG-legal at every event. The format is volatile after Chaos Origins and Revolution Booster released back-to-back.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Lead with the least committal search: Toon Table of Contents into Toon Bookmark is safer than activating Toon Kingdom blind when you also want Bookmark in GY as destruction cover.
  • 2Do not overpay Kingdom protection — it banishes from the top of your Deck face-down. Count your deck before protecting every small Toon in grind games.
  • 3Toon Page-Flip wants different names: reveal Red-Eyes Toon Dragon, Toon Dark Magician, and Toon Black Luster Soldier when possible; any hit is useful and two of them extend.
  • 4Choose the Toon Black Luster Soldier mode before the Battle Phase: it either banishes a card or attacks that turn, not both. If direct damage is lethal, ignore the removal effect.
  • 5Toon World the Perfect World's temporary banish happens before another activated effect resolves — it dodges removal aimed at a Toon body, but never saves the field spell itself.
  • 6Mind Scan declares a card name not activated in that chain. Use revealed-hand knowledge to name the opponent's next engine piece, not the current chain link.
  • 7Respect Droll & Lock Bird — it is common in the current main-deck ecosystem. With field access already secured, search protection before greedy extension.

Recovery Options

  • 1GY protection: Toon Bookmark in GY protects a Toon World you control from destruction by card effect — not banish, bounce, or negation.
  • 2Monster loop: The Black Stone of Legend recycles a Red-Eyes monster from GY back to Deck on a later turn, then returns itself to hand for another starter.
  • 3RV01 grave rebuild: Dark-Eyes Illusionist Faceless Mage places Mind Scan or recovers a Toon World-mentioning monster from GY; Blue-Eyes Toon Ultimate Dragon shuffles Fusion materials from hand, field, or GY and adds Toon cards back from GY each turn.
  • 4Search reload: after a wipe, prioritize field access first via Toon Table of Contents, Toon Bookmark, or Terraforming, then Toon Page-Flip or Red-Eyes lines. A hand full of monsters without Toon World text is not a recovery.

+ Side Deck Options And Counters

Matchup / problemSide optionsHow to use them
Backrow and field-spell hate
Cosmic Cyclone banishes problem backrow and field spells. Harpie's Feather Duster and Lightning Storm clear set cards before Toon Kingdom or Toon World the Perfect World commits.
Combo decks
Use low-commitment hand traps because your own engine needs spells. Droll & Lock Bird is strongest when the opponent needs multiple searches.
Graveyard decks
Evil Box puts a GY card on the bottom of the Deck. D.D. Crow and Ghost Belle & Haunted Mansion are clean single-card answers. Dimension Shifter is strong only when your build can give up GY loops.
Going second into boards
Break interaction with Evenly Matched or Forbidden Droplet, then use Comic Hand and direct attackers to win without fully clearing the board.
Control mirrors
Royal Decree is a high-risk trap shutoff if you trim your own traps. Field Barrier protects field spells from destruction but not banish or bounce.

What Stops You

  • 1Cosmic Cyclone, Twin Twisters, Evenly Matched, and any banish or bounce effect on the field spell that Toon Bookmark cannot replace.
  • 2Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring on Toon Bookmark or Toon Page-Flip; Infinite Impermanence on Toon Dark Magician or Comic Cat; Nibiru, the Primal Being after the fifth summon.
  • 3Ghost Belle & Haunted Mansion on Dark-Eyes Illusionist Faceless Mage or Blue-Eyes Toon Ultimate Dragon recursion; D.D. Crow on Toon Bookmark when they can remove the field spell afterward.
  • 4Opponents who keep removal for Toon Black Luster Soldier before the Battle Phase, or who remove Comic Hand's equip target.
  • 5OCG tech (Road of the King report): Droll & Lock Bird is the single most effective card against Toon but is currently Limited. Mulcharmy Purulia is the preferred maindeck hand trap — the deck's many summons easily give it 2-3 draws. Gordian Slicer cannot be negated and pierces Mind Scan, Toon Terror, and Baronne de Fleur.
  • 6Field-spell hate that beats Bookmark: PSY-Framegear Delta overcomes Toon Bookmark protection, and Ghost Ogre & Snow Rabbit hits the Perfect World ② search (and Mind Scan ②). Veidos the Eruption Dragon destroys the field spell on activation but fails if Bookmark is already in GY.

How You Counter Back

  • 1Hold Toon Terror specifically for effects that hit Toon Kingdom or Toon World the Perfect World — that exchange matters more than any monster trade.
  • 2Load Toon Bookmark into GY early so targeted destruction on the book is never a clean one-for-one.
  • 3Called by the Grave and Crossout Designator protect the critical Bookmark or Funny Dark Rabbit activation from hand traps.
  • 4When their removal is spent, Comic Hand and direct attackers convert any leftover monster into lethal instead of fighting through it.
  • 5Against Gordian Slicer, the Expansion build leaves threats in GY and re-summons them with Charmer Quartet in Bloom ② after Slicer resolves, beating its Main-Phase-only window; the Midrange build simply holds threats in hand.
