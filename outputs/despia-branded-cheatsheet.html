Playstyle And Win Condition

Midrange Fusion control. You win by trading one card for multiple pieces of value, then converting a simplified board into Mirrorjade pressure or a Fusion push.

1 Identity

Resource engine first, combo deck second

Modern Branded is less all-in on a single Branded Fusion. White Dragon, Nadir, Branded in High Spirits, and The Fallen & The Virtuous all convert Extra Deck sends into setup or removal.

2 Pressure

Layered interruptions

Typical layers are Mirrorjade banish, The Fallen & The Virtuous pop or revive, Forbidden Crown freeze, Super Polymerization, Mercourier, and Branded Retribution.

3 Close

Grind into lethal

Albion, Cartesia, BIHS, Quem, Branded Sword, and Dragon Dogma reload the hand. Once the opponent is low on cards, Mirrorjade plus a second Fusion or Super Poly usually ends the game.

Current example package

From the referenced YGOPRODeck list: 40 main, 15 extra, 15 side. Key ratios include 2 Aluber, 2 Fallen of the White Dragon, 1 Branded Fusion, 3 Branded Opening, 3 Branded in High Spirits, 3 Nadir Servant, 3 The Fallen & The Virtuous, 3 Forbidden Crown, and 3 Super Polymerization.

Sequencing Tips

Small order choices matter more than a memorized line.

Before committing Branded Fusion

  • Check whether White Dragon, BIHS, or Nadir already gives you a playable board. Branded Fusion is limited, so it is strongest when it resolves through a weak response window.
  • If using the Saronir route, dump Branded Retribution so it can add Branded Fusion back from the GY.
  • Keep a discard plan for Lubellion. Discarding a card that later recurs is better than throwing away a live interruption.

Choosing Extra Deck sends i

  • Albion: set or add a Branded Spell/Trap in the End Phase.
  • The Dragon that Devours the Dogma: End Phase search for Dogmatika or Tri-Brigade access.
  • Granguignol: sends a Level 6+ LIGHT/DARK monster and later tags out when the opponent Special Summons by monster effect.

Using The Fallen & The Virtuous

  • Mode 1 sends an Albaz Extra Deck monster and destroys a face-up card. This is removal plus future End Phase value if the sent monster has a GY trigger.
  • Mode 2 revives from either GY if an Ecclesia monster is in your field or GY. White Dragon into Incredible Ecclesia turns this on naturally.
  • Because it is a Quick-Play Spell, set it before passing if you want interaction on the opponent's turn.

Crown and Super Poly

  • Forbidden Crown can force a turn by freezing a key monster, but the Crowned monster cannot be used as regular Fusion material that turn.
  • Use Super Polymerization before Crown if that monster must become material. Use Crown first if the monster is a negate you do not need to fuse away.
  • Mirrorjade cannot use its banish effect on the next turn after it resolves, so pair it with TFTV, Super Poly, or Mercourier when possible.

Recovery Options

The deck stays alive by turning graveyard and banished cards back into live cards.

Albion EP set

When sent to GY that turn, Albion sets or adds a Branded Spell/Trap. This finds follow-up or interaction.

BIHS returns

If a Fusion Monster was sent to your GY this turn, Branded in High Spirits can add itself back in the End Phase.

Cartesia loop

Cartesia returns from GY in the End Phase if a Fusion was sent to your GY that turn.

Sword retrieve

Branded Sword recovers a banished Albaz or monster that mentions it, including Mercourier after Gold Sarcophagus.

Retribution recycle

Banish Retribution from GY to add another Branded Spell/Trap from GY, often the lone Branded Fusion.

Quem revive

When a card leaves the Extra Deck, Quem can revive Albaz or an Albaz mentioner from your GY.

Aluber safety net

If your Fusion leaves by opponent effect, Aluber can revive and negate an opponent's Effect Monster.

Dark Dragon shuffle

From GY, Ecclesia and the Dark Dragon shuffles itself, a Level 8 Fusion, and a field card into the Deck.

Example side cards from recent lists

Kewl Tune
Bring in: Droll, Mistaken Arrest, Dark Ruler, Droplet, Purulia when hand summons matter.
Plan: reduce hand knowledge payoff by having multiple live engines. Do not rely only on Branded Fusion.
Branded mirror / Dracotail
Bring in: Bystial Magnamhut, D.D. Crow or Ghost Belle if available, Super Poly, Illusion Gate, Crown.
Plan: hit Albaz, Cartesia, or the revival window. Crown White Dragon, Cartesia, Arthalion, or Mirrorjade when it buys the turn.
DoomZ / combo boards
Bring in: Dark Ruler No More, Forbidden Droplet, Lightning Storm, Super Polymerization, Forbidden Crown.
Plan: break first, then establish Mirrorjade plus follow-up. TFTV is valuable because it removes a face-up card while loading GY value.
Backrow / stun
Bring in: Feather Duster, Lightning Storm, Cosmic Cyclone if you expect floodgates.
Plan: force backrow before committing Cartesia or Branded Fusion. Save Crown for floodgate monsters, not passive traps.
What counters you
Watch for: Ash on Branded Fusion or Nadir, Droll, Dimensional Barrier, D.D. Crow/Bystials, Necrovalley, Dimensional Fissure, Anti-Spell.
Answer: diversify into White Dragon, BIHS, Nadir, and set removal. Use Called By/Crossout only when the exchange protects a real route.
