Midrange Fusion control — checked June 7, 2026

Despia Branded

A top-to-bottom cheatsheet for the 2026 Dogmatika / Branded / Despia shell. The focus is branch selection: when to use the one-card Fallen of the White Dragon spine, when to cash in Branded Fusion, and how to turn Extra Deck sends into follow-up.

TCG, checked 2026-06-07 Role: midrange Fusion control Win: trade up, grind into Mirrorjade lethal Example: Rochester WCQ Regional Top 4

+ Playstyle And Win Condition

Game Plan

Resource engine first, combo deck second. Modern Branded is less all-in on a single Branded Fusion: Fallen of the White Dragon, Nadir Servant, Branded in High Spirits, and The Fallen & The Virtuous all convert Extra Deck sends into setup or removal. You win by trading one card for multiple pieces of value, then converting a simplified board into Mirrorjade the Iceblade Dragon pressure or a Fusion push.

  • 1Layer interruptions: Mirrorjade banish, TFTV pop or revive, Forbidden Crown freeze, Super Polymerization, Tri-Brigade Mercourier, and Branded Retribution.
  • 2Reload the hand with Albion, Cartesia, BIHS, Quem, Branded Sword, and The Dragon that Devours the Dogma.
  • 3Once the opponent is low on cards, Mirrorjade plus a second Fusion or Super Poly usually ends the game.

Current Example Package

From the referenced YGOPRODeck list: 40 main, 15 extra, 15 side. Key ratios include 2 Aluber the Jester of Despia, 2 Fallen of the White Dragon, 1 Branded Fusion, 3 Branded Opening, 3 Branded in High Spirits, 3 Nadir Servant, 3 The Fallen & The Virtuous, 3 Forbidden Crown, and 3 Super Polymerization.

Branded Dracotail is the latest topping variant: Dracotail Faimena and Dracotail Lukias add extra Fusion volume, Dracotail Arthalion bounces, and the Dracotail traps recycle while drawing.

White Dragon spine

One-card line when the Fusion route is committed or stopped: send Albion or Dogma, summon Incredible Ecclesia, Synchro into Dark Dragon, and turn on TFTV revive mode.

++

BIHS / Nadir dump engine

Hand-fixing toolbox: BIHS reveals a Dragon or Spellcaster, sends a Level 8 Fusion with 2500 ATK or DEF, and searches an Albaz mentioner; Nadir sends Dogma, Albion, or Titaniklad and searches the next body.

++

Gold Sarcophagus resource route

Gold Sarcophagus banishes Mercourier, which searches Albaz or a mentioner (White Dragon is the cleanest). Later, Branded Sword recovers the banished Mercourier as live interaction once an Albaz Fusion is up.

++

Branded Dracotail branch

The latest top variant: Faimena discards to fuse, Lukias searches a Dracotail on summon, materials set Sting/Flame/Horn, Mululu can negate a face-up monster, and Arthalion bounces per hand material and revives itself when two or more monsters hit the GY at once.

i Tips And Recovery

Sequencing Tips

  • 1Before committing Branded Fusion, check whether White Dragon, BIHS, or Nadir already gives a playable board — it is limited and strongest through a weak response window. On the Saronir route, dump Retribution so it can add Fusion back from the GY.
  • 2Pick Extra Deck sends deliberately: Albion sets or adds a Branded Spell/Trap in the End Phase, Dragon Dogma searches Dogmatika or Tri-Brigade access, and Granguignol sends a Level 6+ LIGHT/DARK and later tags out when the opponent Special Summons by monster effect.
  • 3The Fallen & The Virtuous: mode 1 sends an Albaz Extra Deck monster and destroys a face-up card; mode 2 revives from either GY if an Ecclesia monster is in your field or GY (White Dragon into Incredible Ecclesia turns this on naturally). It is a Quick-Play, so set it before passing for opponent-turn interaction.
  • 4Forbidden Crown freezes a key monster without allowing monster responses, but the Crowned monster cannot be regular Fusion material that turn — use Super Polymerization first if that monster must become material, or Crown first if it is a negate you never need to fuse away.
  • 5Keep a discard plan for Lubellion: discarding a card that later recurs is better than throwing away a live interruption.

Recovery Options

  • 1Albion sent to the GY that turn sets or adds a Branded Spell/Trap; BIHS and Cartesia both return in the End Phase if a Fusion was sent to your GY this turn.
  • 2Branded Sword recovers a banished Albaz mentioner (including Mercourier after Gold Sarcophagus); banish Retribution from GY to add another Branded Spell/Trap, often the lone Branded Fusion.
  • 3Quem revives Albaz or a mentioner when a card leaves the Extra Deck; if your Fusion leaves by opponent effect, Aluber can revive and negate an opponent's Effect Monster.
  • 4From the GY, Ecclesia and the Dark Dragon shuffles itself, a Level 8 Fusion, and a field card back into the Deck.

What Stops You

  • 1Ash Blossom on Branded Fusion or Nadir Servant, plus Droll & Lock Bird against your search chains.
  • 2GY and banish hate: D.D. Crow and Bystials like Magnamhut on Albaz or Cartesia.
  • 3Floodgates: Dimensional Barrier, Necrovalley, Dimensional Fissure, and Anti-Spell Fragrance.
  • 4Generic answers worth recognizing: Infinite Impermanence, Ghost Belle, and Nibiru on longer Fusion turns.

How You Counter Back

  • 1Diversify into White Dragon, BIHS, and Nadir so no single negate ends the turn.
  • 2Carry set removal for floodgates and pace your searches around Droll.
  • 3Use Called By / Crossout only when the exchange protects a real route, not just any starter.
